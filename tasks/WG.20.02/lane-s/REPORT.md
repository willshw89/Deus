## What changed

Hand-written inputs (the build reads them; it never writes them):
- `art/catalogue/geometry.json`: the contract geometry (32 layers, z -16..15, tile 48, layer 96, `stratumPx` [19,19,19,19,20], frame classes, optional params OFF, bands) with a `decisions[]` row per value (source file:line and DECIDED/OPEN).
- `art/catalogue/catalogue.schema.json`: JSON Schema 2020-12, `$id` `deus-art-catalogue/1.1.0`.
- `art/catalogue/strip_transcription.json`: the scale-strip labels read from `art/reference/DEUS_HUMAN_SCALE_STRIP_V1.png`, with the strip's sha256. Labels only; no pixels copied.
- `art/catalogue/size_inputs.json`: the brief's race drawn heights and SRD file/entry references, checked by the build against the SRD text and 7 px/ft.
- `art/catalogue/mapping.json`: source-id to category/scale-row/ramp mappings; rows with no chart row or ramp that names the thing are marked PROPOSED (Owner question Q-SCALE-MAP).
- `art/catalogue/reference_inputs.json`: reference paths with kind, tracked, ownerApproved, thirdParty and styleAnchorEligible.
- `art/catalogue/conflict_claims.json`: the text claims the build locates in sources to produce file:line pairs in conflicts.md.
- `art/catalogue/.gitattributes`, `docs/art/catalogue/.gitattributes`, `tools/art/fixtures/catalogue/.gitattributes`: `* text eol=lf`, so generated outputs are byte-identical under any `core.autocrlf`.
- `docs/art/catalogue/SCHEMA.md`: field-by-field schema doc, geometry and size-class rules, and the terrain list used.

## Not done / known problems

1. **The catalogue goes stale when main edits a pinned source, and main already has.** `catalogue.json` pins the sha256 of every source (55), and `conflicts.md` cites file:line. Since the base, main commit 79038709 (`[gemini] Record WBS Rev 25 ...`) edited `docs/worldgen/DEUS_WORLDGEN_WBS.md`, which conflicts.md cites. `git merge-tree` reports a clean textual merge, but on the merged tree `--check` fails. Raw output from the simulation (clone at 20e52e00, WBS taken from origin/main 9f9954e5):
   ```
   DIFF art/catalogue/catalogue.json
   DIFF art/catalogue/conflicts.md
   CHECK: FAILED (2 file(s) differ from a fresh build)
   EXIT=1
   ```
   The rebuild changes 3 lines: the WBS sha256 in `sources[]` and two line numbers in conflicts.md (NM-02 :678 -> :679, ST-01 :718 -> :719). `test_catalogue.js` would fail `catalogue.rebuild_identical` for the same reason. Remedy after a merge: `node tools/art/build_catalogue.js` (it writes only generated files under `art/catalogue/` and `docs/art/catalogue/`), then both gate commands again. I did not merge main into this branch (the brief says do not merge). Every future edit to a pinned source, including coordinator docs such as the WBS files, OWNER_DECISIONS.md and VISION.md, has the same effect. See Decisions needed.
2. `reference/u7_shapes_0_31.png` (untracked, third-party) is absent from the worktree and from every clone, so it is `UNVERIFIED_ABSENT` (a warning, as the brief specifies) and has never been hashed in this lane. The other 26 references are tracked and were re-hashed by `catalogue.references_hash`.
3. 2476 painted entries use a PROPOSED scale row or palette ramp (354 source entries, 2122 addendum placeholders), because no chart row or ramp names the thing. They are listed per entry (`mapping.scaleBasis` / `mapping.rampBasis`) and raised as Q-SCALE-MAP; nothing is decided.

5. All 25 biomes are unnamed (OWNER_OPEN), so every entry uses biome `SHARED`. The `<BAND>_B1..B5` placeholders exist but no entry uses them yet (Q-BIOMES).
6. Interface art (window skins, menus, cursors, markers, badges) is out of scope with a reason, because the scale chart has no size rows for it (Q-UI). Those rows account for part of the 243 outOfScope rows.
7. No entry is APPROVED: `art/APPROVALS.md` rows predate DEC-007 and are not treated as approvals (Q-APPROVED).
8. DEPTH_<band> recolours make up 7595 of the 10089 entries. They carry no colours and no paint slots (checked by `catalogue.depth_no_colours`). Whether this is the intended scheme is Q-DEPTH.

## Owner questions (copied from `art/catalogue/conflicts.md` §9; asked, not answered)

1. **Q-BIOMES** Which 25 biomes exist and which 5 belong to each band (LOWER2, LOWER1, SURFACE, UPPER1, UPPER2)? Until then every entry is SHARED and the placeholders <BAND>_B1..B5 are unused.
2. **Q-BANDS** Are the band ranges (LOWER2 -16..-9, LOWER1 -8..-1, SURFACE 0..3, UPPER1 4..9, UPPER2 10..15) and the z range -16..15 final?
3. **Q-LEGACY-Z** Legacy "z = -2 Deep Caverns" content (AR-1905..1908) lands in LOWER1 by numeric containment, not in LOWER2 "Deep Caverns". Which band should it be in?
4. **Q-STRATA** Is the stratum split [19,19,19,19,20] final, and are strata listed bottom-up? (Slot sizes use the tallest run of k strata, so the order does not change them; target heights use the lowest k strata, so the order changes those.)
5. **Q-FACINGS** Edge strips and ramp side faces are catalogued for facings N/E/S/W with the same 48-px width and stratum-derived height. Are N/E/W vertical faces drawn at all in the top-down view, or only S faces plus top-edge lips?
6. **Q-H5-FULL** Height difference 5 strata and the full-layer face have the same pixel height by default. Keep both slots, or merge them?
7. **Q-RAMP-SLOT** Ramp cells are sized tilePx + rise of k strata (PROPOSED). Is that the drawing convention?
8. **Q-DEPTH** Tile art (top tiles, edges, ramps, ramp sides, connectors) is painted once in its home band and appears in the other bands as DEPTH_<band> recolours with no paint slot and no colours. Is that right, and which colours does each DEPTH palette get?
9. **Q-TOP-VS-A2** Surface terrains have both an RMMZ A2 autotile block and a DEC-019 top-surface tile. Is the A2 block the top-surface tile set, or are both needed?
10. **Q-HANGING** Hanging props (roots, vines, stalactites, waterfalls, dust, light shafts) are painted per band in every band, including the sky bands; no source rules any of them out. Which bands should drop which kinds?
11. **Q-LIGHT** Deep light sources are the source-cited emitters (mapping.lightSources) recoloured per band. Which light sources should each band have, and should they be recoloured at all?
12. **Q-ALPHA** Rim shadows, height-shading overlays, dust, light shafts and ore/hole overlays carry alphaMode OWNER_OPEN (binary until ruled). Is partial alpha allowed for them?
13. **Q-DECAY** Decay overlays are one 48x96 overlay per wall material and stage per band. Should they match every piece of the wall set (16/20 pieces) instead?
14. **Q-FRAMES-HUGE** Frame sizes for HUGE and GARGANTUAN creatures (no paint slots until set); and whether LARGE_LONG 96x48 (PROPOSED) is accepted.
15. **Q-TALL-MEDIUM** Turn on TALL_MEDIUM (48x64) and the Gnome/Halfling readability floor (26-28 px)? Both are OFF.
16. **Q-SRD-OPEN** Accept the PM pixel readings of open-ended SRD heights (Dragonborn 46-48, Human/Half-Orc/Tiefling 35-46, Elf 33-44) and the PROPOSED widths of races with no registry row?
17. **Q-CHILD** Baby and teen sizes (only CHARACTER_CHILD exists in the chart). Child entries cite the SMALL frame row and carry the CHARACTER_CHILD height in notes; per-race child heights are not charted.
18. **Q-PEOPLE** Are goblin, orc and automaton (people briefs, cultures) people, monsters or neither under the DEC-013 rule of exactly nine races? They are catalogued as MISSING character entries until ruled.
19. **Q-SNOW** The registry forbids snow/ice biomes but the WorldCatalog has snow, ice, icy water, snow fir, snow bush, arctic fox, ice wraith, glacier and tundra. Keep or drop them? (Catalogued as the sources name them.)
20. **Q-SCALE-MAP** 2476 painted entries use a PROPOSED scale row or palette ramp (354 source entries, 2122 addendum placeholders; no chart row or ramp names the thing; see mapping.scaleBasis / mapping.rampBasis per entry and art/catalogue/mapping.json). Confirm or reassign.
21. **Q-LIVING-PAL** The palette registry has no skin, fur, hair or feather ramps; people, creatures, faces and body layers carry placeholder ramps. Which ramps should living beings use?
22. **Q-UI** Should interface art (window skins, menus, cursors, markers, badges) join the catalogue? It needs size rows the scale chart does not have; today it is out of scope with a reason.
23. **Q-APPROVED** art/APPROVALS.md rows predate DEC-007 (several say "approved via review policy" or "Awaiting user approval"). No entry is marked APPROVED until the Owner confirms which approvals stand.
24. **Q-REFS** Which references are Owner-approved (every reference is ownerApproved UNKNOWN), and may the art/masters style anchors serve as style anchors under DEC-007?
25. **Q-TREE-FRAMES** How many sway frames do trees and other swaying props get (AGENTS.md Rule 12)? Tree briefs deliver one frame, so trees have frames.cols = 1.
