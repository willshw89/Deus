## ADDENDUM A1 (Owner ruling 2026-09-26 12:41 CT; issued AFTER the writer launched at 12:40:38 CT, so it is not in the committed BRIEF.md of run lane-al_20260926_124038)
Scope and allowedPaths are unchanged. Add to the Humanoids section of DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md, UF_AssetStandard.json and the validator:
1. **Racial class garb and armour sets (Owner ruling, normative):** each of the 9 races has a default, racially cohesive garb for each of the 12 SRD 5.1 classes (barbarian, bard, cleric, druid, fighter, monk, paladin, ranger, rogue, sorcerer, warlock, wizard), plus light, medium and heavy armour sets: 15 outfits per race, 135 total. 2. **Combat animation approach (PM-proposed, adopt as the standard's default and mark it "PM-proposed, Owner may amend"):** body poses are drawn once per body template (swing, thrust, draw/loose, cast, hit/flinch, dodge/parry, death; 3-4 frames x 4 directions). All garb/armour layers are drawn frame-for-frame on the same pose grid. Weapons and shields attach via per-frame main-hand / off-hand anchors: (x, y) plus one of a small fixed set of angles, each angle pre-drawn (no runtime rotation, DEC-011). Dedicated per-pose frames only for deforming pieces (capes, long robes, large shields, bows mid-draw).
3. **Validator:** checks every layer frame against the body template's pose grid and anchors (frame count, frame size, anchor presence per frame, angle in the allowed set, deforming-piece frames present where required). Add fixtures and killed mutants for these checks.

15. **Tool-side anchor alignment and equipment anchoring (Owner 14:09 + 14:10 CT; issued AFTER the A1-A9 pass started; applied by the A9b follow-up):**
   (a) Generators (e.g. PixelLab) give no anchor or layer guarantees, so **our own tool enforces anchors**. After generation it detects each frame's landmarks from the pixels (transparent-pixel mask, silhouette, foot line, head outline): feet / bottom-centre pivot, head centre, main-hand and off-hand points. It shifts the frame by **whole pixels** so the landmarks land on the slot map's anchor coordinates for that cell, and writes the detected anchors into the slot's serialized record (item 14). Whole-pixel shifts and trimming transparent margins are not scaling, so this fits the no-scaling rule. A frame whose anchor cannot be moved into place without clipping the sprite, or with wrong proportions/size or head drift relative to the body, is **rejected and regenerated**. Optional: template/pose reference images with the body pre-positioned are fed to the generator so the correction is usually 1-2 px.
   (b) **Equipment anchoring:** every body frame stores main-hand and off-hand anchor points, a **per-frame grip angle** (so a blade tilts correctly through a swing) and a **per-direction draw-order flag** (e.g. shield in front of the body facing down, behind it facing up; same for weapons). Weapon and shield layers are drawn **once per grip pose** and pinned to those anchors frame by frame by the compositor, so one weapon/shield design works on every race and body template without being redrawn per animation frame. The anchor tool detects hand positions the same way it detects the feet. The standard defines these fields; the validator checks every body frame has them.
   Note: this goes into the **Deus Art manual** when it is written. No art generation.
