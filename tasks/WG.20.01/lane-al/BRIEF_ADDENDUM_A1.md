## ADDENDUM A1 (Owner ruling 2026-09-26 12:41 CT; issued AFTER the writer launched at 12:40:38 CT, so it is not in the committed BRIEF.md of run lane-al_20260926_124038)
Scope and allowedPaths are unchanged. Add to the Humanoids section of DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md, UF_AssetStandard.json and the validator:
1. **Racial class garb and armour sets (Owner ruling, normative):** each of the 9 races has a default, racially cohesive garb for each of the 12 SRD 5.1 classes (barbarian, bard, cleric, druid, fighter, monk, paladin, ranger, rogue, sorcerer, warlock, wizard), plus light, medium and heavy armour sets: 15 outfits per race, 135 total. A shared silhouette standard per armour weight (light / medium / heavy) applies across races; each race has a palette/motif sheet (ramps and motifs by ID). Encode the 9 x 15 matrix as required-set data (outfit IDs, layer membership, which slots each outfit fills).
2. **Combat animation approach (PM-proposed, adopt as the standard's default and mark it "PM-proposed, Owner may amend"):** body poses are drawn once per body template (swing, thrust, draw/loose, cast, hit/flinch, dodge/parry, death; 3-4 frames x 4 directions). All garb/armour layers are drawn frame-for-frame on the same pose grid. Weapons and shields attach via per-frame main-hand / off-hand anchors: (x, y) plus one of a small fixed set of angles, each angle pre-drawn (no runtime rotation, DEC-011). Dedicated per-pose frames only for deforming pieces (capes, long robes, large shields, bows mid-draw).
3. **Validator:** checks every layer frame against the body template's pose grid and anchors (frame count, frame size, anchor presence per frame, angle in the allowed set, deforming-piece frames present where required). Add fixtures and killed mutants for these checks.

## ADDENDUM A3 (Owner ruling 2026-09-26 12:43 CT, Items; issued AFTER the writer launched)
Scope and allowedPaths are unchanged. Normative, for DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md, UF_AssetStandard.json and the validator:
1. **Items are race-neutral SRD items.** There is one "Plate Armor" with one stats entry and one item ID, never "Elven Plate Armor". This applies to all armour, weapons, shields and class garb.
2. **Race only selects the art variant layer.** Art is keyed by `itemId` + race (e.g. layer key `<itemId>__<race>`; define the exact naming pattern and the fallback when a race variant does not exist yet), so the same item looks elven on an elf and dwarven on a dwarf. Loot, crafting and trade always see one item (one ID, one stat block); race never creates a new item ID.
3. Reconcile with A1: the 135 racial outfits are art variants of race-neutral items/garb IDs, not separate items. Reconcile with the catalogue EQUIPMENT/paperDoll entries and DEUS_Anim's `$UF_Layer_<itemId>` convention (Appendix A/C).
4. **Validator:** flags any race-specific item ID or stat duplication, and checks that equipment layer art keys follow `itemId` + race; fixtures + killed mutants.
## ADDENDUM A4 (Owner ruling 2026-09-26 12:46 CT; issued AFTER the writer launched)
Scope and allowedPaths are unchanged. Normative, for DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md, UF_AssetStandard.json and the validator:
1. **Icons:** a single fixed icon grid and icon size, with a shared outline, light direction and palette. One icon each for every SRD item, material, tool, spell school, condition, status, resource and job. Rarity and quality are shown by a border or corner mark, never by a redrawn icon.
2. **Natural resources:** every resource node has fixed states (full, partly harvested, depleted, regrowing), a harvest animation and a dropped-item sprite. Covers trees, ore veins, stone, clay, herbs, crops, water sources and animal spawns, with a variant per biome (6 biomes, DEC-030).
3. **Animation policy:** everything that can be animated is animated by default; a static asset needs an explicit, listed exception (exception list in the standard/JSON). Minimum animated set: water, lava, fire, smoke, torches and forges; vegetation sway plus seasonal and weather states; weather (rain, snow, fog, lightning); ambient birds and critters; doors, gates, mills and workstations in use; construction and destruction stages; resource working, tree fall and rock collapse; unit idle breathing and blinking; emotes; spell and combat effects; UI pulses. Each animated class gets a fixed frame count and a shared timing clock (define the clock and per-class frame counts). Pixel frames only, DEC-011, no shaders.
4. **Validator:** checks icon grid/size and rarity-as-overlay (no rarity-specific icon art), resource node state/harvest/drop completeness per biome, and flags any asset with no animation and no listed exception; fixtures + killed mutants.

1. **Capture and domestication (Owner 12:59 CT):** enemies and wildlife can be tamed into pets, mounts, livestock and work animals; captured humanoids become prisoners or recruits.    **Owner 13:01 CT correction (supersedes the 13:00 creature-equipment item):** tamed creatures fight in the party using ONLY their basic SRD 5.1 stat blocks and natural attacks and defences. There are **NO creature armour or equipment slots, no barding and no crafted creature gear.** The collar/saddle/harness markers are therefore visual status/riding layers only (no slot, no stats); list "are collar/saddle/harness visual markers wanted at all after the 13:01 correction?" in Appendix B. The validator flags any creature equipment-slot definition.
2. **Minimum variety per biome (PM decision 13:01 CT), for each of the 6 biomes:** trees 4 species x 3 variants (young, mature, old or dead) = 12, each with harvest and seasonal states; bushes/shrubs 4 (1-2 harvestable); ground-cover scatter 6; rocks 3 sizes x 2 variants = 6; ore/mineral nodes 2-3 (biome-specific); water-edge pieces 3; unique landmarks 2 (about 40 surface pieces per biome, about 240 base pieces total). Per underground depth band: 4 cave formations, 2 fungi or crystals, 1-2 ore nodes. Seasons are palette swaps; harvest states follow a template; horizontal flip allowed only for pieces without directional lighting. These are MINIMUMS (more may be added); the validator checks the counts.
3. **Per-race hair and face-gene minimums (PM decision 13:02 CT):** hair 12 styles per body type (3-4 race-distinctive) plus 3 balding stages that overlay any style; facial hair 8 (stubble, short, full, long, braided, mustache, goatee, forked), extra for dwarves, skipped for races without beards. All hair recoloured by one ramp: about 12 natural colours plus race-specific ones, and 4 greying stages to white; no per-colour art. Genetic facial features, each a gene with inherited options as swappable face layers (with matching charset cues where visible): face shape 4, eye shapes 5 (colour from a genetic ramp), brows 4, nose 5, mouth/lips 4, ears 3 per race, jaw/chin 3, skin tone as a genetic ramp, markings 4 overlays (freckles, moles, birthmarks), race features 3-4 each (horns, scale patterns, tusks, etc.). Inheritance mixes the parents' genes with rare mutation (the sim owns inheritance; the standard defines loci and layer mapping); age layers sit on top so identity is kept from child to elder. The validator checks the counts per race and body type.
4. **Entity portraits (Owner 13:03 CT, part of A8):** every non-face entity (items, creatures, resources, buildings, workstations, spells) also gets a **144x144 faceset-format portrait** (the detailed inspect / tooltip / crafting view) in addition to its icon. Portraits use a per-category background (per race for race-styled items), the same palette and outline rules, and the A4 rarity/quality marks. Race-neutral items (A3) get one portrait per race art variant. The validator requires BOTH an icon and a portrait for every entity.
