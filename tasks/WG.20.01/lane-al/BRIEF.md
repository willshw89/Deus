## Owner rulings that win over older docs (list every conflict in Appendix A)
- **DEC-011:** flat layer rendering, 1 source pixel = 1 screen pixel, NO filters, NO blur/glow/bloom shaders, no runtime scaling/rotation/tinting as a substitute for drawn frames. Brightness and glow come from drawn frames and palette ramps.
- **DEC-030:** 6 biomes (volcanic, wet, arid, temperate, cold, wild) x 5 depth bands, plus 15 transition sets. Supersedes "5 biomes", "25 biomes/combinations" and "10 transitions" wherever they appear.
- **DEC-013:** 32 Z layers (-16..+15), 1 cell = 5 ft, 1 layer = 10 ft, 5 strata x 2 ft per layer, nine races.
- **DEC-027:** SRD 5.1 is the rules authority; V64 is retired.
- **DEC-016:** the scale chart governs sizes.
- **Owner 12:38 CT (DECIDED, not an open question): 4 directions, not 8, for ALL character and creature sprites (RMMZ standard rows S, W, E, N).** The ~150 existing 8-way sheets (sidecar facings S,SW,W,NW,N,NE,E,SE) are mined for their straight-direction frames only; diagonals are not part of the standard. The validator flags 8-direction sheets as non-standard (legacy source, mine S/W/E/N rows).
- **Owner 12:39 CT:** spells are composed from standard parts, not per-spell animations (see Effects).

## Required content of DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md
### 1. Global rule set (applies to every asset)
48 px grid; DEC-011 1:1, no filters; master palette (`art/palette/deus_master_world_palette_v1.hex` and `uf.hex` per ADR-002; reconcile which is canonical) and per-biome / per-faction / per-damage-type colour ramps by ID; outline rules (colour, thickness, selout); single light direction (state it; reconcile with the charter/VFX doc); shadow rules; layer order (ground, strata, objects, units, equipment layers, effects, UI) consistent with DEUS_Anim SLOTS; naming conventions (reconcile catalogue ids `BAND_BIOME_CATEGORY_TYPE_VARIANT_STATE`, runtime file names, `$`/`!` prefixes, sidecar names); RMMZ-compatible layouts: charsets 3 columns x 4 rows per character, 8 characters per standard sheet (`$` = single-character sheet, `!` = no-offset object), faces 144x144 in 4x2 sheets (576x288), SV battler layout (RMMZ 9x6 motions of 64x64 frames; state whether DEUS uses SV battlers and for which categories), RMMZ generator conventions (part/layer names, colour-ramp gradient conventions) mapped to DEUS generator parts; DEUS extensions in RMMZ-compatible form: Large frames 48x96 (tall) and 96x48 (long), extra animation rows as additional `$` sheets or sidecar-declared rows, never breaking RMMZ's 3x4 walk read.
### 2. Per-category required sets (each a checklist the validator can test)
- **Critters (Tiny/Small, SRD size based):** idle, walk, attack, hurt, death rows (4 directions); optional fly / swim; corpse sprite; harvest/loot icon; NO face; frame size by SRD size class.
- **Beasts/monsters Medium+:** critter set plus per-attack combat rows (bite/claw/slam/breath etc. from the SRD stat block), condition overlays, SV battler, Large frame shapes (48x96 tall / 96x48 long / larger multiples).
- **Humanoids (the 9 SRD races: human, elf, halfling, dwarf, gnome, dragonborn, half-elf, half-orc, tiefling):** paper-doll body template per race/sex/body type with FIXED anchors per frame (feet, head, both hands, main-hand and off-hand weapon anchors, back, belt) recorded in data; full part layers (body/skin, eyes, hair front/back, beard, ears/horns/tail/scales where racial) and gear layers matching DEUS_Anim's 18 slots and the catalogue paperDoll layers (extend the 34 human T0 entries to all races/body types); faces that match the charset, including worn armour and helmet; continuous age steps (child .. elder) with wrinkles and face-shape change; gradual genetic hair greying ramp natural -> salt-and-pepper -> grey -> white and inherited balding patterns; genetics-visible part variety (extend today's 5-locus genetics; inheritance rules belong to the sim, the standard defines the visible loci and their part/ramp mapping); action rows: melee swing, thrust, bow draw/loose, crossbow aim/fire/reload, thrown, casting one-handed / two-handed / focus, crafting and work loops (hammer, saw, chop, dig, stir, carry, kneel), hit / flinch / dodge / parry / death; RMMZ-style balloon emotes; visuals for all 15 SRD 5.1 conditions (blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, incapacitated, invisible, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, stunned, unconscious) as pose, frame-freeze, palette ramp swap, decal or icon, all DEC-011 compliant (no runtime alpha/tint shaders unless drawn frames/ramps; state how Invisible is shown without a filter). Map charter families F01-F14 onto these rows, 4 directions each.
- **Biomes:** 6 biomes x 5 depth bands + 15 transition sets; for each: ground autotiles, cliffs, multi-layer ramps/slopes (one 10-ft layer over 5 squares), water, vegetation, props, seasonal variants, transitions, colour ramp.
- **Race/faction building styles:** the SAME piece list for every faction (walls, roofs, doors, windows, floors, furniture, workstations, construction stages, damage/ruin states), styled per the six profiles in DEUS_FACTION_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD.md; show how the 9 races map to profiles/cultures (today only 7 cultures and 6 profiles exist: list the gaps, do not invent lore).
- **Items/icons:** one icon grid (size, sheet layout, index scheme); every SRD weapon, armour, material, tool, spell school, condition and status gets an icon slot.
- **Effects:** see the spell composition below; plus projectile, impact, fire, water, weather; frame counts; damage-type colours.
- **UI:** balloons, selection rings including multi-layer selection, HP bars, lower-layer overlays, faction window skins.
### 3. Effects: spell composition (Owner-approved 12:39 CT; normative)
Spells are composed from standard parts, never per-spell animations. Four phases:
1. **Cast pose** (one-hand, two-hand, focus raise, channel) + school-coloured hand glow (drawn frames per school ramp).
2. **Delivery shape** mapped to SRD area shapes: projectile/bolt, beam, cone, line, sphere/burst, cylinder, self-aura, touch, instant-at-target. Each is ONE standard animation with fixed frame counts and sizes on the 48 px grid.
3. **Impact sprite per SRD damage type** (13: acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, piercing, poison, psychic, radiant, slashing, thunder), each with its own colour ramp.
4. **Lingering effects handed to the simulation** (fire tiles spread, cold freezes, force/thunder damage structures, spheres cross Z layers) plus a looping aura/icon for concentration, buffs, curses and wards.
Each SRD spell is defined as data: cast pose + delivery shape + damage type + sim effect, in the spell-visual table (`game/data/UF_SpellVisualTable.schema.json`). Optional custom pieces for iconic spells may be added later (schema hook only). DEC-011: no blur/glow shaders; brightness comes from drawn frames and ramps. Effects on other Z layers must display correctly in layer view (define how).
