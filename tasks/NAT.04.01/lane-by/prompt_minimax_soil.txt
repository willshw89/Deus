## 3. Physical & Mathematical Calibration

### 3.1 2-ft Strata Soil Horizons
- 5 discrete strata per 10-ft Z cell ($s \in [0..4]$), volume $5\text{ ft} \times 5\text{ ft} \times 2\text{ ft} = 50\text{ cu ft}$ per stratum cell.
- Soil types:
  - **Horizon O / A (Topsoil)**: upper stratum ($s = s_{\text{top}}$), organic fraction $\ge 2000\text{ bp}$, bulk density $\approx 3750\text{ cp/cu ft}$.
  - **Horizon B (Subsoil)**: intermediate stratum, clay/silt enrichment, bulk density $\approx 4750\text{ cp/cu ft}$.
  - **Horizon C (Regolith / Saprolite)**: lower stratum directly overlying unweathered bedrock, bulk density $\approx 6000\text{ cp/cu ft}$.
- Composition tracked as integer basis points ($0 \dots 10000$): `sand`, `silt`, `clay`, `organic` (sum must equal 10000 bp).

### 3.2 Dynamic Moisture & Capillary Coupling
- Soil moisture $m \in [0 \dots 10000]\text{ bp}$ tracks pore saturation.
- Capillary draw: Unsaturated soil strata draw moisture from saturated strata beneath (coupling with `NAT.03.01` aquifer) up to field capacity ($\approx 3500\text{ bp}$ for loam).
- Gravitational percolation: Moisture exceeding field capacity drains downward into lower permeable strata or cracks under gravity.
- Canonical undirected edge signed residual tracking:
  $$\text{grossSignedFlow} = \text{calculatedSignedFlow}_{A \to B} + \text{residual}[\text{edgeKey}]$$
  $$\text{integerFlow} = \text{truncTowardZero}(\text{grossSignedFlow})$$
  $$\text{residual}[\text{edgeKey}] = \text{grossSignedFlow} - \text{integerFlow}$$
  Ensures zero water lost to integer truncation.
- Receiver-side clamping: Transfers must not exceed available pore capacity or cause oversaturation (>10000 bp).

### 3.3 Sediment Transport & Angle of Repose
- Loose sediment observes material friction angle $\theta$:
  - Gravel: $35^\circ$
  - Dry Sand: $34^\circ$
  - Moist Loam: $45^\circ$ (cohesion)
  - Saturated Mud / Clay: $20^\circ$ (liquefaction risk)
- If vertical step to adjacent cell exceeds stable angle of repose threshold, unsupported loose mass cascades down-gradient into the lower cell. Mass is 100% conserved into ledger.

### 3.4 Weathering, Thermal Degradation & Closed Mass (DEC-040)
- Mechanical/chemical rock weathering: Exposed bedrock in contact with water/frost slowly fractures top stratum into mineral regolith ($C$-horizon):
  $$\Delta \text{Rock} = -\Delta \text{Regolith}$$
  in exact integer centipounds.
- Water-driven erosion: High fluid flow across surface strips topsoil into suspended sediment, depositing alluvial loam downstream.
- Thermal degradation: Contact with molten lava ($Z \in [-16 \dots -11]$) bakes clay into ceramic and vaporizes organic topsoil into carbonaceous ash/gas, conserving mass into atmospheric/sediment accounts.

### 3.5 Event-Driven & Quiescent Operation
- Zero per-frame full-world loops.
- Undisturbed soil is quiescent.
- Disturbed/hydrated cells mark dirty flags. Only dirty cells update during simulation ticks until steady state.
