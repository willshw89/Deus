### MAJOR-1: the water family must be booked in du (required)
The ledger books the `water` family in fluid depth units (`game/js/sim/ledger_defaults.js:29`; du is defined at `:11`). `tasks/WG.65.15/lane-l1/LEDGER_API.md:49` says the ledger never converts units and families never mix. SIM.40.01 A9.1, A9.2 row 19 and A9.7 (`tasks/SIM.40.01/lane-q/SIM.40.01_STRUCTURAL_SUPPORT.md` ~:673, ~:702, ~:736) say an ice voxel holds exactly 1 du and weighs 1,011 kg (its support load). Those three sources agree, and the catalogue is the only thing that differs. The unit of the water family is therefore **not** an open Owner question. Only the size of `mu` (1 g, 1 kg or 1/16 lb, question 1) is still open. So the PM ruling for this fix is:
1. **Book every water-family amount in du.** The `ice` record books 1 du per slice. `massOf("ice", "strata", 1)` and every yield, collapse, thaw and reclamation posting of class `water` carry du amounts, never mu. Keep 1,011 kg (and its mu equivalent under the proposed scale) as a separate support-load field, for example `supportLoadKgPerSlice` / `supportLoadMuPerSlice`, cited to SIM.40.01 A9.1. It is never posted to the ledger.
2. **Add a unit/family check to the validator.** Every posting's amount unit must equal its class family's ledger unit (du for `water`, mu for everything else, following `ledger_defaults.js`). Also check that a record's `ledger.unit` equals its family's unit. Add negative fixtures (for example an ice posting in mu, and a mineral posting in du) and a killed mutant for the check.
3. **Fix the `lava` label** (`materials.json` ~:2244). Class `lava` is family `mineral`, booked in mu after the fixed du-to-mu ratio (`LEDGER_API.md` ~:354). Set `ledger.unit` to `mu` and keep `muPerDu` as the documented conversion.
4. **Repeat the reviewer's demo** with the real `game/js/sim/ledger.js`, read only. Register one full fluid cell `water/fluid 7` and one ice slice `water/ice` at `massOf("ice","strata",1)`, seal, then thaw that one slice. Paste raw output showing the water family total rises by exactly 1 du (7 to 8) and that every family balances. 5. **Record it in REPORT.md** as disagreement `D-WATER-DU` (catalogue vs ledger/SIM.40.01, resolved by PM ruling FIX1 in favour of the ledger's du). Name every changed record and posting count.
