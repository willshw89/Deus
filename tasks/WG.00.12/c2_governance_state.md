## Interpretations for Review (Grok, please attack these)
- The non-terminal allowlist accepts aliases: TODO ← PLANNED, QUEUED, OPEN, NEW, BACKLOG, NOT_STARTED, PENDING, PROPOSED, DRAFT, REOPENED; IN_PROGRESS ← ACTIVE, WIP, STARTED, ASSIGNED, ONGOING, IN_WORK; REVIEW ← IN_REVIEW, UNDER_REVIEW, AWAITING_REVIEW, PENDING_REVIEW, READY_FOR_REVIEW, FIX_READY, VERIFY_PENDING, VERIFY_REQUEST, CHANGES_REQUESTED; BLOCKED ← ON_HOLD, SUSPENDED, PAUSED, WAITING, DEFERRED. Without them the live workflow (`PLANNED` leaves, `OPEN` → `FIX_READY` defects) would need closure verification for every step.
- The first phrase of a status cell must be exactly a known status: `QUEUED behind 19A` is a closure; `QUEUED (behind 19A)` is not.
- `A ← B` means A (B was the old state); `A ↔ B` makes both A and B candidates.
- Grandfathering covers 4.4 only, because the brief places it under §4.4. The epoch is `a12f94a7`; the coordinator moves `GOVERNANCE_EPOCH` forward to the hook-install commit when the hook is installed.
- A cited review artifact must mention the record's ID and be last committed by the closer. It is not required to live in the closer's whitelist (the range gate's 4.4 check on the artifact's own commit covers that), and its verdict is not read.
- A reachable commit hash remains sufficient 4.1 evidence, as in Lane C2; the review artifact carries the independent check.
- Prose claims are judged per sentence: every ID in a sentence with a non-negated closure word is claimed. "WG.00.09 stays in REVIEW until WG.00.07 is DONE" claims nothing (`UNTIL`).

## Known Limits
- Identity is declared, not authenticated: a commit subject `[grok]` is trusted as Grok's. `--range` catches such a commit only through 4.4 on the paths it touches.
- A negator within three words before a closure word cancels it (`not pending, DONE` is caught; `not pending DONE` is not). Prose skips hyphenated closure words (`DONE-ish`).
- Homoglyphs outside NFKC (Cyrillic `О` in `DОNE`) are not folded; they make the value unknown, which counts as a closure.
- `git commit --amend`, `--no-verify` and hooks living in the shared common `.git/hooks` are as described for Lane C2; DEC-004 is still `OPEN`, and the hook is **not installed**.
- The range gate trusts `Merge branch '...'` subjects for lane hints; a hint can only narrow a commit to one lane, never widen it (an agent that does not hold the hinted lane is rejected).
