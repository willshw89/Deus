## Governing geometry and scale (Owner rulings; corrects every 9-layer assumption in draft 0019-S)
- World: 32 layers, default z range -16..+15 with surface at 0 (DEC-013 as amended, addendum §13). Nothing in the catalogue, templates or tools may assume 9 layers or hard-code a layer count; read it from `art/catalogue/geometry.json`.
- Sim geometry: one square = 5 ft; one layer = 10 ft; 5 strata of 2 ft per layer (addendum §12).
- Pixel geometry (Owner, 01:55 CT 2026-09-26, relayed by PM): tile = 48 px = one 5-ft square. Layer face height = 96 px (2 tiles). Stratum pixel split default = [19, 19, 19, 19, 20] px (sums to 96). The stratum split is a PARAMETER in `geometry.json` (`stratumPx`), never a literal in code: every tool reads it, and a test must show that changing it (for example to [20,19,19,19,19] or [24,24,24,24,0]-style invalid input) changes slot heights or is rejected.
- Humans stay deliberately small: about 42 px tall on the 48-px grid (the scale chart's HUMAN 42), so imposing monsters fit a 2-tile frame. Human-scale props (doors, beds, tables, chairs, fences, chests) are sized to the 42-px human per the scale chart, NOT to true feet. The scale chart (strip `art/reference/DEUS_HUMAN_SCALE_STRIP_V1.png` + its numeric source `game/data/DEUS_ScaleRegistry.json`) is the size authority for sprites and props (DEC-016 in docs/OWNER_DECISIONS.md). Sim distances govern the simulation only.
- Character-size authority (PM 01:59 CT, derived from SRD 5.1 text in `game/data/srd51/character_options.json` race "Size" traits and the `game/data/srd51/rules.json` Size Categories table): creatures are drawn at 7 px per foot (a 6-ft human = 42 px) on the 48-px, 5-ft grid. The sim keeps the true SRD heights; these are drawn heights only.
  - Race drawn heights (all in 48x48 frames): Dragonborn 46-48 px; Half-Orc, Human and Tiefling 35-46; Elf 33-44; Half-Elf 35-42; Dwarf 28-35; Gnome 21-28; Halfling about 21. Undecided (Owner): a readability floor of 26-28 px for Gnome and Halfling with slightly larger heads is a parameter with default OFF (see below), never applied silently.
  - Sprite frame vs footprint (Owner correction, 02:04 CT): the SRD space (e.g. Large = 2x2 squares) is the combat/footprint area in the sim and is stored SEPARATELY from the sprite frame. Every creature/character entry carries both `footprint` (squares, from SRD size) and `frame` (pixels, from the frame class below). Never derive one from the other.
  - Frame classes: `TINY` = 24x24 footprint drawn in a 48x48 frame; `SMALL` and `MEDIUM` = 48x48 frame (1 tile); `LARGE_TALL` (tall-bodied Large: humanoids such as ogres and trolls) = 48 wide x 96 tall frame on a 2x2 footprint (Owner); `LARGE_LONG` (long-bodied Large: horses, big beasts) = 96 wide x 48 tall frame on a 2x2 footprint (PM proposal consistent with the Owner's call; `status: PROPOSED`). Large frames are NOT 96x96. `HUGE` (3x3 footprint) and `GARGANTUAN` (4x4+ footprint) follow the same tall/long principle but their frame sizes are TBD by the Owner: store them as parameters (`frame: null`, `status: OWNER_OPEN`, `future: true`) and generate no paint slots for them until set.
  - Undecided parameters, default OFF: `TALL_MEDIUM` (a 48x64 frame for Medium creatures up to 8 ft) and the Gnome/Halfling readability floor (26-28 px with slightly larger heads). Both exist in geometry/size data as parameters with `enabled: false`; nothing uses them unless the Owner turns them on, and a test shows that turning one on changes the affected slots.
  - RMMZ character sheet blocks are 3x4 frames: 48x48 -> 144x192; 48x96 (LARGE_TALL) -> 144x384; 96x48 (LARGE_LONG) -> 288x192. Larger blocks (e.g. 288x384, 432x576, 576x768) only once the Owner sets Huge/Gargantuan frames.
- Biome bands (PM defaults, OPEN for the Owner): LOWER2 (deep) -16..-9, LOWER1 (shallow underground) -8..-1, SURFACE 0..+3, UPPER1 +4..+9, UPPER2 +10..+15. 25 biomes = 5 bands x 5 biomes. The biome-to-band assignment and the 25 biome names are OWNER_OPEN: never invent names. `game/data/DEUS_BiomeRegistry.json` lists 5 canonical biomes (TEMP, WET, ARID, HIGH, VOLC) and forbids snow/ice; record the 5-vs-25 gap in conflicts, and use neutral placeholder ids `<BAND>_B1..B5` (flag `ownerOpen: true`) where a per-biome slot is needed before the Owner rules.

- `catalogue.json` top level: `schemaVersion`, `tileSizePx: 48`, `geometry {path, sha256}`, `palette {path, sha256}` (art/palette/deus_master_world_palette_v1.hex), `scaleChart {path, sha256}`, `sources[] {path, sha256, role}`, `sheets[]`, `entries[]`, `outOfScope[] {sourceId, reason}`.
- `sheets[]`: `{sheetId, kind: ATLAS|RMMZ_TILESET|RMMZ_CHARACTER, group {band, biome, type}, w, h, gridPx, runtimeFile|null}`. ATLAS sides are multiples of 48 and at most 4096. RMMZ sheets use the fixed sizes in docs/RMMZ_ASSET_SPEC.md (A1/A2 768x576, A3 768x384, A4 768x720, A5 384x768, B-E 768x768; `$` character sheets 3 x frameW by 4 x frameH).
- `entries[]`: `id` (`BAND_BIOME_CATEGORY_TYPE_VARIANT_STATE`), `category`, `band`, `biome`, `zMin`, `zMax` (inside geometry range), `sizeClass` (SRD size) and `frameClass` (for characters/creatures; footprint and frame stored separately), `sourceIds {catalog[], assetIndex[], brief[], ar[], manifest[], matrix[], addendum[]}`, `scaleRow` (required; a scale_chart.json row id, or a geometry row id such as `GEOM_TILE`, `GEOM_STRATUM_1..5`, `GEOM_LAYER_FACE`, `GEOM_FRAME_LARGE_TALL`, or a `size_classes.json` race row such as `RACE_HUMAN`), `envelope {wMin,wTarget,wMax,hMin,hTarget,hMax}`, `footprint {w,h}` (tiles), `anchor {type: GROUND|CEILING|WALL|CENTER, x, y}`, `paletteRampIds[]`, `frames {cols, rows, facings, rate}`, `slot {sheetId, slotId, x, y, w, h} | null` (slotId = `<sheetId>:<4-digit index>`), `runtime {kind, file, index|tileId}`, `variants {derivedFrom|null, flipH, flipV, rot, paletteSwap|null, lightingSafe}`, `paperDoll {...}|null`, `references[]`, `standardPending|null`, `alphaMode: BINARY|OWNER_OPEN`, `status: MISSING|EXISTING_UNAPPROVED|STAND_IN|STOCK|APPROVED|OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `statusWhy`.
- Slot sizing rule: slot w = 48*ceil(wMax/48), h = 48*ceil(hMax/48) per frame; geometry-derived pieces (edge/cliff strips, wall faces, ramps) take their heights from `stratumPx`/`layerPx`, never from literals. Variant rows (`derivedFrom` set) own no paint slot (`slot: null`).

## Deliverables
1. `docs/art/APPROVALS_FORMAT.md`: the Owner, and only the Owner, appends to `art/APPROVALS.md` a section `## SHA-256 approval ledger (v1)` with the table `| Date | Decision (YEA/NAY) | Entry or slot ids | File (repo path) | SHA-256 (64 lowercase hex) | Approved derived variants (ids or none) |`. The tools read ONLY that section. Legacy rows without a hash are ignored (legacy art stays unplaceable until the Owner adds ledger rows). A NAY row, a malformed hash, or two rows that disagree for one hash means refuse.
2. `tools/art/validate_art.js <png> --entry <id> --catalogue <path> --approvals <path> [--geometry <path>] [--json]` refuses (exit != 0, reporting pixel coordinates) when: the file's sha256 is not in a YEA row for that entry; file dims != slot w x h (e.g. 47x48 rejected); any alpha not 0 or 255 (unless the entry's `alphaMode` is OWNER_OPEN and the Owner has ruled; until then refuse); any opaque pixel not in `deus_master_world_palette_v1.hex`; any pixel equal to a template grid/label/background colour (template residue; read the colours from the template sidecar); SCALE: the opaque bounding box per frame outside the chart row's [wMin..wMax] x [hMin..hMax]; ANCHOR: for GROUND anchors the lowest opaque row is not the frame baseline (frameH-1), for CEILING anchors the highest opaque row is not row 0; a tile-class slot not 100% opaque; a geometry-derived slot (edge strip, wall face, ramp) whose height does not match `stratumPx`/`layerPx` from geometry.json (never a literal).
3. `tools/art/place_art.js --catalogue <path> --approvals <path> --in <dir> --out <dir> [--replace <slotId>]`: validates each input first, copies pixels 1:1 into the exact slot rect, exports runtime RMMZ sheets per `entries[].runtime`, writes a coverage report (filled, empty, unexpected slots per sheet and per band) and `placement_report.json`. Never resamples, recolours or edits pixels; refuses to overwrite a filled slot without `--replace <slotId>`; rows with `derivedFrom` are reported `DERIVED_PENDING` and nothing is written; deterministic output (sha256 equal on rerun); updates no catalogue file.
4. `tasks/WG.41.01/lane-u/REPORT.md`: commands with raw EXIT lines, the mutant kill table, provoked-failure evidence, final `git rev-parse HEAD`.
