# VISION: what we're building

Working title: **UF**. Not final: "Ultima" is a trademark, so it can't be in the release name.

## In one paragraph
A living, randomly generated world simulated with Dwarf Fortress depth (terrain, materials, gathering, construction, needs, jobs, combat with body-part wounds). It's shown and played like Ultima VII: the U7 2.5D view, objects you can pick up, drag, and open, and inhabitants with daily lives. Built in RPG Maker MZ using plugins only.

| V46 | **The world moves on a beat**, 1 beat per second at normal speed (to start; tunable): a unit steps one cell per beat, work and needs advance per beat, AI decides on beats; the speed keys play more beats per second; pause stops the beat. |

| V48 | **Every living thing makes or follows a decision every beat.** Each beat, each unit (colonist, creature, person) either continues its current decision (next step, next stroke of work) or makes a new one; "stand here" counts. | V43 | **Colonies have DF's full range of labors and a military**: mining, woodcutting, carpentry, masonry, farming, cooking, brewing, fishing, hunting, animal care, leather/cloth/bone crafts, smithing, hauling, building, healing, and soldiers; every faction site has walls with gates and guards on them, and the player's colony assigns labors and squads like DF. |
| V10 | Engine first. All the U7 and DF functionality works (with graybox placeholder art) before any unique art is generated. |
| V51 | **Every faction grows its society on its own in play** (user, 2026-09-18 night): its people forage, gather, build rooms, beds and houses, reproduce and raise children, and expand, the same as the player's colony does; the player's orders only steer the player's faction. | V52 | **Every faction has a power structure** (user, 2026-09-18 night): people hold ranks, and those with rank direct those below them (a ruler at the top, then leaders of sites, squads and work groups, then everyone else). | V54 | **100 years pass on the generated map before the game starts** (user, 2026-09-18 night): after the world and its 500–600 years of history are generated and the map is built, the living simulation (V51: every faction foraging, gathering, building rooms, beds and houses, reproducing, growing) runs for 100 years on the real map, coarsely and within the generation budget, so the player takes control of a world that has already been lived in: sites grown or fallen, people born, aged and died (V40), ranks settled (V52), stockpiles and buildings in place. |

| V56 | **Inside dwellings, creatures lay flooring** (user, 2026-09-18 night): once a room is walled, its inhabitants floor it cell by cell as a plan step, with the material their culture prefers (planks from logs, flagstones from stone, rushes from straw); a floored room counts as a better room (a good thought for those who sleep there, as DF values rooms), and bare earth inside a dwelling is something to fix. |
| V57 | **Doors** (user, 2026-09-18 night: "let's add floors and doors"): every gap in a site's ring and every house entrance gets a door of the culture's material; a door opens for its faction and allies and stays shut to animals and strangers (DF keeps the wildlife out with doors), can be built by the player like a wall, can be locked later with U7-style keys, and can be broken in combat. |
| V11 | The user sees and approves every art asset before it goes into the game. |
| V12 | Work happens in vertical slices. The user approves each slice before the next starts. |

## Open questions (only the user can answer)
Don't guess these. If a task depends on one, ask.
- **Q1 Time:** real-time with pause and speed control (DF and U7 both work this way), or something else?
- **Q2 Population:** how does it grow from two people? Births, arriving migrants, or both?
- **Q3 Failure:** is there a lose condition, or does the world just carry on?
- **Q4 Control:** does the player ever steer one unit directly (U7-style), or only give orders (DF-style)?
- **Q5 Facings:** do sprites have 4 facings (like U7) or 8? This roughly doubles the art. See `ART_STANDARD.md` §4.
- **Q6 Scale:** pixel scale and screen size. See `ART_STANDARD.md` §2.
- **Q7 World size in areas:** how many areas across and down? The default is 6×6, which at the proposed scale is about Ultima VII's world size (U7 was 3072×3072 of its own tiles = 1536×1536 of our cells). It's a plugin parameter, so it's easy to change.
- **Q11 Life timescale:** at 1 game hour per real minute, a DF-length year would take over 100 real hours. How long should a year, a pregnancy, and a childhood be in real play time?
- **Q12 Injuries:** with U7-style combat, also keep DF-style injuries (bleeding, broken or lost limbs, infections), or pure hit points?
- ~~Q9 One world system~~: resolved 2026-09-18, a fresh seeded world (see the Decision log).
- ~~Q10 Who builds colonist AI and factions~~: resolved 2026-09-18, Claude Code (engine).
- **Q13 Height (asked 2026-09-18):** build up in the U7 way (an elevation per cell, walls and stairs with height, up to about three storeys, hills and cliffs with ramps) after the world build lands, with no digging down (the underground layer is retired)? Claude Code's recommendation: yes. This is construction step 4 plus the projection fix K3.
- **Q8 Area edges:** crossing an edge currently switches the screen to the next area (DF-style). A seamless, U7-style continuous scroll is possible later, but it's harder in RMMZ. Keep switching for now?
