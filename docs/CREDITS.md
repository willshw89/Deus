# CREDITS: third-party material and the attribution the game must show

## System Reference Document 5.1 (d20 rules, VISION V47 and V55)

The combat and crafting rules use rules text and tables from the SRD 5.1 under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. The licence requires the following attribution, shown exactly as written:

> This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

What is taken (as of 2026-09-18), all recorded in `docs/design/COMBAT_CHAINS.md` and `game/data/UF_WorldCatalog.json`:
- **"Weapons" table (Equipment → Weapons):** the damage dice and properties of the club, dagger, handaxe, spear, mace, shortsword, longsword, battleaxe, warhammer, shortbow, longbow, sling and light crossbow, and the property definitions (ammunition, finesse, heavy, light, thrown, two-handed, versatile), including normal and long ranges.
- **"Armor" table (Equipment → Armor):** the armor class of leather, hide, chain shirt, ring mail, chain mail and plate, the shield's +2, the Dexterity-modifier caps of medium armor and the Strength requirement of heavy armor.
- **Ability scores and modifiers, proficiency bonus, attack rolls, critical hits, damage rolls, unarmed strikes, advantage and disadvantage, saving throws** (Using Ability Scores; Combat → Making an Attack; Combat → Damage and Healing).

Nothing else from the SRD is used: no spells, monsters, classes, races, magic items or setting text. Item names in the game are generic ("Short sword", "Iron mail"); no D&D product-identity names appear (AGENTS.md → Reference vs. shipped content).

## Not shipped (for completeness)
- Ultima VII shapes are visual references and development stand-ins only (`U7_`-prefixed files, `docs/STATUS.md` → Stand-ins); they are replaced by original art before any release and are not credited here because nothing derived from them ships (AGENTS.md rule 8).
- Dwarf Fortress is a mechanics reference only (`docs/design/DF_MECHANICS.md`); none of its text, data or raws is used.
