# UF_Roads
Roads between the factions' living sites, plank bridges where a road crosses water, 2–4 farm plots beside the road outside every living site and one well inside its ring, all generated with the map from the seed (a UF_World generator that runs after UF_WorldGen has laid the ground and stamped UF_History's sites). Roads are the ground kind `road` drawn by UF_Tiles; bridges, farm plots and wells are catalog objects drawn by UF_Objects. Mechanics and structure follow the two reference games (sites with zones and farm plots; a world with roads, bridges, fields and wells), implemented originally.
Status: built 2026-09-18 (night), checks: `roads` (11 checks, all PASS on a snapshot copy, see Checks). Not yet registered in the real `game/js/plugins.js` (the Register task does that; until then the catalog entries are inert data and nothing draws roads).

## How it generates (generator `uf_roads`, order 20, runs in `UF.World.buildArea` after `uf_worldgen` at 10)
1. **Sites:** `UF.History.sitesIn(ax, ay)`; living = `faction && !ruined && kind ∉ {lair, ruin}`. The home is the site with `protected: true` (else `UF.History.homeSite()`, else the first living site). No living site → nothing is generated (`lastBuild.skipped`).
2. **Cost grid** from the built area: land 1, an existing road 0.7 (so later roads join earlier ones into a network), a walk-over object (`passable: true`) 1 and it is removed, a blocking plant (tags `tree`/`bush`/`plant`) 4 and it is removed, any other blocking object (walls, buildings, boulders, ore, pillars, campfires) impassable, rock (region 250 or a ground kind with `passable: false`) impassable. Inside any site's disc (Chebyshev `radius + 1`, every site kind) nothing is ever removed or painted over: walk-over pieces (straw beds, stockpiles, rubble) cost 1 and stay, walls block, so a road enters a site only through a gap in its ring. Objects protected by a minimum are never removed below it: the start kit (`start.kit.objects` within `kit.radius[1]` of the map centre) and the map kit (`start.mapKit.objects[].min` over the area); each removal spends one unit of that class's allowance (count − minimum).
3. **Roads:** Kruskal over the living sites (edges sorted by Euclidean distance, ties by site id); each edge that joins two components is routed with A* (4-connected, deterministic tie-break on the cell index, Manhattan heuristic at cost 1, so lightly weighted against the 0.7 road cost). The route starts on the 4 neighbours of the site's centre cell and ends when it is 4-adjacent to the other centre (the centres hold the hearth). **Water:** a step into water becomes a straight jump across the water run in that direction, allowed only when the run is ≤ 5 cells (`MAX_BRIDGE`; rivers are 3–5 wide) and lands on passable land; it costs 20 per water cell (1 where a bridge already stands). A* stops at `NODE_CAP` 40 000 expansions per road (`lastBuild.capHits` counts it; measured 0 hits, 7 500–15 000 expansions for all roads together). After the spanning set, any site whose component doesn't contain the home is routed straight to the home (this is how "each site's connection to the player's home" is read: the spanning set already connects every site to the home unless a route failed; a direct connection is only added for those). Failed routes are listed in `lastBuild.failed`.
4. **Painting:** along each route, a water cell gets a `bridge` object (a crossing = a run of consecutive bridge cells, listed in `lastBuild.bridges`); a land cell has its removable object removed and, when it is then empty, its layer-0 tile set to the road kind. Path cells inside site discs that hold a piece stay as they are. The road's autotile shape joins road cells **and bridge cells** (so the road runs flush onto the bridge); every land neighbour of a road cell is re-shaped with UF_WorldGen's rule (same kind, counting the ground under water and outside the area as its classified kind through `UF.WorldGen.cellInfoLocal`). Shapes come from `UF.WorldGen.autotileShape` when it exists, else from the same derivation over `Tilemap.FLOOR_AUTOTILE_TABLE` re-implemented here.
5. **Farm plots:** per living site, `2 + floor(unit(seed, 0xfa12, siteId) × 3)` plots (2–4), each a rectangle of 4 cells along the ring side × 3 deep (either orientation) of `farm_plot` objects, on land cells at Chebyshev distance `radius + 2 … radius + 4` from the centre (the band widens to +6 then +8 when land is short), never on water, road, another site's disc, a blocking object, a protected object, or a cell of any culture's colony plan (`catalog.colony.plan`/`plans` build cells relative to the centre). Candidates are scored by how many of their cells touch a road (4-neighbourhood) and taken best first; plots keep one free cell between them; a plot with no road beside it is taken only while fewer than 2 stand. `lastBuild.farms[].byRoad` says which ones touch the road.
6. **Wells:** one `well` object per living site on the free land cell nearest the centre (Euclidean, ties by row then column) at Chebyshev distance 2 … `radius − 1`, not on a road or a colony-plan cell (fallback: any free interior cell at distance ≥ 2). No well only when the interior is full (`lastBuild.failed` says so).
7. **Bookkeeping:** every object the generator removes or adds is booked in `UF.WorldGen.stats["ax,ay"]` by object id, so worldgen's `objects_placed` stays as true as it was before roads (`lastBuild.statsDelta === added − removed`).

Nothing here uses the beat, `UF.Time` or `Graphics.frameCount`: the generator is pure (seed → world) and the only runtime hook is passability. Randomness: `UF.World.hash32` only (the farm count); everything else is a deterministic function of the built area.

## API (`UF.Roads`)
| Member | Description |
|---|---|
| `KIND` | `"road"` (the ground kind id in `catalog.groundKinds`) |
| `GENERATOR` | `"uf_roads"` (registered with `UF.World.registerGenerator`, order 20) |
| `config` | `{ costs: { ground 1, road 0.7, passable 1, plant 4, water 20, bridge 1 }, maxBridge 5, nodeCap 40000, budgetMs 300, farm: { along 4, deep 3, bands } }` (read-only constants) |
| `lastBuild` | `{ area: {x, y}, ms, sites, living, roads: [{ from: siteId, to: siteId, cells: [cellIndex …] (route order, from → to), bridges: n }], roadCells, bridges: [{ x, y, cells: [cellIndex …] }], farms: [{ site, x, y, w, h, byRoad }], wells: [{ site, x, y }], failed: [{ from, to, reason }], expanded, capHits, removed, added, statsDelta, skipped: string | null }` of the most recent build of any area |
| `stats["ax,ay"]` | `{ roads, roadCells, bridges, farms, wells, ms }` per area from its last build |
| `network(ax, ay)` → build record or `null` | The build record of that area's latest build with the current seed (same shape as `lastBuild`); cells are indices `y × size + x` |
| `roadBase()` → tile id or `null` | `UF.Tiles.groundBase("road")` |
| `isRoadTile(tileId)` → bool | Any of the 48 shapes of the road kind |
| `isRoadAt(area, x, y)` → bool | Reads `UF.World.getTile` (off-screen areas through the peek cache) |
| `bridgeTypeId()` → number | Type number of `bridge` (0 when the catalog lacks it) |
| `bridgeAt(x, y)` → bool | The cell of the map on screen holds a bridge |
| `bridgeAtIn(area, x, y)` → bool | Any area (`UF.World.getObject`) |
| `autotileShape(same)` → 0–46 | UF_WorldGen's helper, or the built-in derivation when it is absent |

Passability: `Game_Map.prototype.isPassable` is aliased: a cell whose object is a bridge is passable in every direction, whatever its water tile's flags say (UF_Tiles forces water impassable). `UF.World.cellFree` still counts water cells as not free, so nothing spawns on a bridge; UF_Jobs' stand cells exclude water too, so nobody works standing on one.

## State it saves
None. Everything is recomputed from `UF.World.state.seed` on every area build; changes players make later are UF_World's `diffs` / `objectDiffs` as for any tile or object. `lastBuild`, `stats` and `network()` are caches rebuilt by every build.

## Events
Emits none, listens to none. Hooks: `UF.World.registerGenerator("uf_roads", generate, 20)`; `Game_Map.prototype.isPassable` (alias); `Scene_Boot.prototype.start` (alias, registers the suite in test mode).

## Keys and mouse
None. Roads, bridges, plots and wells show in the look tooltip and right-click menu through the existing UF_Look/UF_Interact paths (the road reads as ground kind "Packed earth"; the objects by their catalog names).

## Known limits
- One-area world: sites in other areas aren't connected across area edges (each area routes its own sites; a multi-area world would need edge portals).
- A site with no living site in its area gets nothing; a living site hemmed in by water on every side may get fewer than 2 plots (the check reports it; not seen in 7 runs).
- Bridges are straight and at most 5 cells; wider water is routed around (or the site stays unconnected and is listed in `lastBuild.failed`). Lakes narrower than 6 cells can be bridged too.
- Roads inside a site are painted only on empty cells; walk-over pieces (beds, stockpiles) on the path stay and keep their ground.
- The road kind is a code-drawn placeholder (`UF_GenGround_A2`); the bridge, plot and well tiles are stock RMMZ (see Assets used) and the plot's crop tile is drawn under units because the plot is `under`.
- `UF.WorldGen.stats` over-counts on its own: UF_WorldGen clears a Euclidean disc of `radius + 1` around each site but stamps its ring on the Chebyshev square, so ring corners can overwrite a plant and both are counted (residual 15–86 per map, seed dependent). `worldgen.objects_placed` fails for that reason with or without this plugin; the fix belongs in UF_WorldGen (clear the Chebyshev square, or decrement when a piece overwrites).
- Nothing uses the roads yet: units path with RMMZ's `findDirectionTo` (UF_Movement8D's A* within 12 steps) and don't prefer roads; a road-aware long-range pathfinder for UF_World's off-screen movement is later work.
- The farm plot is a placed object only; sowing, growth and harvest (VISION V43) are a later build.
