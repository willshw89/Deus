# UF_Levels
The persistent levels of the world (VISION V80, `docs/design/VERTICAL_WORLD.md`), one for each level of the world's **Z range** (WG.00.17, `docs/systems/DEUS_ZRange.md`): new worlds have 32 layers, -16..+15; a save made before WG.00.17 has five, `-2`, `-1`, Ground, `+1`, `+2`. All are 256×256 at the same x,y. The core -2..+2 is generated as before. The layers below it are solid rock, and the layers above it open air (plus the mountain rock that rises above +2). Every cell of every level has a **shape** (`solid`, `floor`, `open`, `ramp`, `stairUp`, `stairDown`, `stairBoth`) and a **material** (`stone`, `soil`, `wood`). A level is a seeded **baseline** (regenerated from the world seed, never saved) plus sparse saved **changes**. Since DEUS-TSK-FABLE-19A (2026-09-24) the one stored truth is five **strata** per cell (2 ft each since WG.00.17, DEC-013; 1 ft before); shapes and materials are derived from them (section Strata). Since DEUS-TSK-FABLE-19B (2026-09-25) new worlds are generator 5: natural cuts and cave networks on the five core levels, carved into those strata (section Natural cuts and caves). One level is on screen at a time: each level has its own RMMZ map id, and the levels other than the ground are painted from their shapes with runtime tileset 92. The player moves the view up and down with keys or the level plate; the cursor cell and the camera stay where they were. Old saves load unchanged on the ground.

Status: recovered vertical slice 1 of `docs/design/VERTICAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` (§5), with generator version 2 added 2026-09-19 for mostly-earth underground maps with separated habitable pockets. Structural layer stack, lateral support, and collapse rules are specified in [`docs/systems/DEUS_WORLD_STRUCTURAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`](DEUS_WORLD_STRUCTURAL_ARCHITECTURE.md). Checks: suite `vertical` (11 checks, not in the default run). Stairs/routes and digging/support remain separate work; the baseline generator does not implement them.

**Owner:** Claude Code (engine) · **File:** `game/js/plugins/UF_Levels.js` · **Load order:** after `UF_Fire`, before `UF_Test` (`@base UF_World`; after UF_WorldGen, UF_Tiles, UF_Objects, UF_Items, UF_Jobs, UF_Combat, UF_TimeSpeed, UF_Camera, UF_Look).

The z support lives in the files it belongs to (the "z seam"): `UF_World.js` (map ids, builds, units, paths, off-screen walking), `UF_Objects.js`, `UF_Items.js`, `UF_Jobs.js` and `UF_Look.js`. Their additions are listed below under "Levels in the other plugins". They work without UF_Levels (everything defaults to the ground); UF_Levels adds the levels' contents, the view switching and the migration.

## Coordinates
- A **record** carries `z` beside its area: `unit.z`, `item.z`, `job.target.z`, `job.stand.z`, a regrow entry's `z`, a unit's `goal.z`. Missing = the ground (0).
- An **API handle** may carry `z` inside the area: a *level area* `{ x, y, z }`. Every UF_World, UF_Objects, UF_Items and UF_Jobs function that takes an area accepts one; positional UF_World calls take a trailing `z`.
- **Map ids:** `MapIdBase + slot × areas + area index`, slot 0 = Ground (so the ground keeps id 1000 and old saves don't change), 1 = `+1` (1001), 2 = `+2` (1002), 3 = `-1` (1003), 4 = `-2` (1004). Beyond them, +z takes slot 2z − 1 and −z slot 2z (+3 → 5, −3 → 6, …, +15 → 29, −16 → 32): the same in every world (`UF.World.mapIdSlot`).
- **Fail-closed legacy calls:** `UF.World.currentArea()`, `areaOfMapId()` and `isAreaMap()` answer only for the ground: while another level is on screen, `currentArea()` is `null`. `world:tileChanged`, `world:objectChanged`, `objects:changed` and `world:areaBuilt` fire only for the ground. So a plugin that doesn't know about levels either works on the ground or pauses (see Known problems); it never reads a `-1` map as if it were the ground.

## Levels in the other plugins (the z seam)
**`UF.World`** (every trailing `z` defaults to the ground):
- `LEVELS`, `zOf`, `isLevel`, `levelKey`; `inWorld(ax, ay, z)` (false for a level outside the world's Z range); `areaMapId(ax, ay, z)` (0 for such a level); `levelOfMapId(mapId)` → `{ x, y, z }`; `isWorldMap(mapId)`; `viewLevel()` → the level on screen (a frozen shared object).
- `registerGenerator(name, fn, order, { levels })`: default `[0]`, so every existing generator stays ground-only; `levels` may be a function z → bool (UF_Levels' terrain painter: every level of the range but the ground); `ctx.z` says which level is built. `buildArea(ax, ay, z)`: the start template only on the ground.
- `setTile(…, tileId, z)`, `getTile(…, layer, z)`, `setObject(…, type, z)`, `getObject(ax, ay, x, y, z)`; `setDerivedTile(ax, ay, x, y, layer, tileId, z)` (patches the built maps, records no diff: level tiles are derived from shapes).
- `peekArea(ax, ay, z)` (least recently used eviction, 9 builds since WG.00.17), `adoptBuild(ax, ay, z, map)`, `cachedBuild(ax, ay, z)`, `refreshUnitEvents(map, ax, ay, z)`, `reconcileEvents()`.
- `cellFree(ax, ay, x, y, ignoreUnitId, z)`, `nearestFreeCell(…, ignoreUnitId, z)`, `spawnCellFor(…, name, z)`, `standerAt(ax, ay, x, y, z)`. The level-aware Doors wrapper receives every level; the obsolete bypass for non-ground doors is removed.
- `addUnit({ …, z })` (throws for a level outside the Z range; `u.z` is always written); `unitsInArea(ax, ay, z)`; `sendUnit(id, { area, x, y, z })` returns `false` for a goal on another level than the unit's; `moveUnitToLevel(unit, z, x, y)` (drops goal and path, moves the event on or off screen, emits `world:unitLevelChanged(u, fromZ, toZ)`); `isDisplayed(u)` compares the level.
- `findPath(area, …, { z })`, `walkable(ax, ay, x, y, { z })`, `reachable(levelArea, …)`, `transferView(ax, ay, x, y, dir, z)`.
- **Off-screen walking:** units on a level that isn't on screen follow planned paths on their own level (they used to step in a straight line through trees, walls and water), one cell per `UnitStepFrames`, spread over the frames by unit id; they wait for a unit in the way, then walk round it. `pathConfig.offscreenPaths` (default `true`) switches it off (tests). Fliers keep the straight step.
- **Map loading:** the map leaving the screen stays in the peek cache; a level shown before is shown again from there (unit events renewed) when the view comes from another level, so returning to the ground doesn't rebuild it. The same level again (a map reload, a return from another scene) is still rebuilt, and a build belongs to one world state (a loaded game never shows the previous world's map).
- Events: `world:levelTileChanged`, `world:levelObjectChanged`, `world:levelBuilt(levelArea)`, `world:unitLevelChanged`.

**`UF.Objects`:** all area functions take a level area; regrow entries carry `z`; `objects:levelChanged(levelArea, x, y, fromId, toId)` for levels other than the ground; the "on screen" calls (`at`, `set`, `find`, `apply`, `describe`) use the level on screen. **`UF.Items`:** items carry `z`; the ground index key is unchanged; `create`/`count`/`find` accept `z`; the sprites and "on screen" calls use the level on screen; a removed unit's items drop on its level. **`UF.Jobs`:** targets and stands carry `z` (a target without an area takes the level on screen); `take` offers a unit only jobs on its own level until slice 2; stand cells on another level off screen use `UF.World.walkable` on that level. **`UF_Look`:** the tooltip works on every level; on a level other than the ground its second line is `UF.Levels.describeCell` and sites aren't named.

## Baselines and underground biomes

Generator 2 is used for new worlds. Ground's existing generator/checksum and the open-air geometry of +1/+2 are unchanged by this underground change. Every level's complete shape/material arrays are allocated at `world:initializing`, before founders and kit placement. Every level's baseline exists (the core's arrays; the levels outside the core UNIFORM chunks) even though only one is rendered. Simulation of the other maps must continue.

- `-1`: mostly solid earth, with 36 separated irregular oval chambers. Four jittered Voronoi substrate regions use rooted loam, clay bed, chalk/karst, and shallow cave labels from RESOURCE_ATLAS. Loam/clay/shallow-cave substrate uses soil; chalk/karst uses stone. Biome boundaries are irregular rather than tile-aligned rectangles.
- `-2`: mostly solid stone, with 16 separated larger caverns. Its distinct regions are deep mine belt, crystal cavern, fossil bed, and deep salt cavern. These are natural geological regions, not historical mines or generated structures.
- Each natural chamber has a dry founding square and a four-cell freshwater pool toward its eastern edge. Adjacent chambers are disconnected; mining or future connectors must create routes. The solid border is two cells wide.
- Runtime baseline metadata adds `biome` and `water` byte arrays and pocket descriptors. None is stored wholesale in a save. Generator-2 underground checksums include shapes, materials, biome codes, and water. Sparse edits were packed shape/material data until DEUS-TSK-FABLE-19A; they are now strata records (section Strata).
- Floor art uses existing cave-floor/dug-soil/dug-stone slots by biome. Solid earth distinguishes existing soil and rock looks. Freshwater pools use stock `Outside_A1` water, block land passage, and are recognized by `Tilemap.isWaterTile`. Biome labels appear in Look descriptions.

Measured from the actual generator source on 2026-09-19, before camp carve or resource placement, seed 424242 (65,536 cells per level):

| Level | Solid | Disconnected chambers | Floor cells per chamber | Freshwater cells | Minimum dry founding square |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| -1 | 54,742 (83.5297%) | 36 | 275–337 | 144 | Radius 5 (11×11 cells) |
| -2 | 54,738 (83.5236%) | 16 | 603–762 | 64 | Radius 7 (15×15 cells) |

Biome cells on -1: rooted loam 15,372; clay 16,548; chalk/karst 17,315; shallow cave 16,301. On -2: deep mine belt 14,946; crystal cavern 17,556; fossil bed 16,163; deep salt cavern 16,871. Seeds 0 and 1 also yielded exactly 36/16 disconnected chambers and 83.21–83.48% solid terrain; every advertised founding square was checked to be dry floor. These are generator measurements, not rendered runtime evidence.

Generator 1 remains unchanged for saves whose `levels[z].gen` is 1: original noise pockets, materials, and checksum bytes. Those baselines have no biome/pool/pocket metadata. Loading does not move an existing faction into new chambers. A pre-V80 Ground-only save receives new underground baselines through its existing migration without changing Ground.

Limits: four biome families per depth are a bounded subset of RESOURCE_ATLAS. Labels do not assert populated crystals, fossils, salt deposits, hazards, plants, or inhabitants. Surface-column alignment, aquifers, magma, mining jobs, routes, and support/falling are separate systems. Kits must select actual free dry floor inside a chamber; a radius-20 ring extends beyond shallow chambers.

## Drawing (tileset 92) and the looks
The catalog's `levels.look` (else the same placeholders built into the code) says which sheet draws each look: `rock`, `soil`, `cave_floor`, `mined_stone`, `mined_soil`, `deck_wood`, `deck_stone`, `open_air`, `hole_edge`, `roof_wood`, `stair_up`, `stair_down`, `stair_both`, `ramp_up`, `ramp_top`, `ladder_foot`, `ladder_top`, `vein_iron`, `vein_copper`, `vein_gold`, `vein_gem` (`docs/handoffs/HANDOFF_vertical.md` §2 and §7). At boot (Scene_Boot waits for it, about 50 ms) UF_Levels copies each look, with its tint baked in, into three runtime sheets in the slots of the handoff's §3: `UF_GenLevels_A2` (A2 kinds 0-7: cave floor, dug stone, dug earth, wooden deck, stone deck, open air, hole edge, roof), `UF_GenLevels_A4` (A4 kinds 0-1: rock, soil) and `UF_GenLevels_B` (B tiles 1-11: stairs, ramps, ladders, veins). A sheet that fails to load is drawn as a flat colour and named in a warning. When Gemini's `UF_Levels_*` sheets are delivered and checked, each catalog entry becomes `{ "sheet": "UF_Levels_A2", "slot": "A2", "kind": n }` without a tint; no code changes.

Cell → tiles: layer 0 the biome's base look (autotiled against matching neighbours), layer 1 the hole edge over an underground `open` cell, layer 2 a stair or ramp. Passage flags: rock, soil, open air, hole edge and freshwater pools `0x0f`; dry floors and connectors 0; B tile 0 and veins `0x10`. Tileset 92's A1 slot is stock `Outside_A1`; generated A2/A4/B contracts stay intact. Ground keeps tileset 91 (UF_WorldGen paints it); its cliff faces and its ramps/stairs are sprites drawn from these runtime sheets (next section but one).

## Natural cave walls (2026-09-19)

Exposed `solid` stone/soil cells on -1 and -2 now draw like built walls: a 48x96 sprite has its lower wall face on the blocked base `(x,y,z)` and its upper cap at `(x,y-1,z)`. The cap is visual overhang only; there is no shape, object, collision entry, or geometry on another z. Dry floor north or south remains passable. Interior solid terrain keeps its existing A4 plateau tiles.

The renderer assembles the existing runtime A4 stock cap and face quarters using native floor/wall autotile tables. Soil and stone retain their distinct source art/tints, and floor biomes are unchanged. No image assets or generated pixel art were added. Visible boundary sprites are pooled, their material/connection bitmaps cached, and they are direct tilemap children sorted by their base foot like UF_Objects: a unit north of a wall is behind its cap; a unit south appears in front. Rebuilds inspect only the viewport plus a small margin when scrolling, switching maps, or changing a shape. `Spriteset_Map.createCharacters` is aliased to add this visual layer; it stores no save state.

Focused non-default suite `natural_walls`: `two_cell_render` checks both materials' 48x96 frames, opaque cap/face pixels, and same-level cap offset; `one_collision_cell` checks blocked base, passable north/south floor and no object duplication; `material_and_depth` checks distinct material bitmaps and north/wall/south sort order; `no_errors` checks harness errors. Screenshot `two_cell_natural_walls` includes connected soil and stone runs beside stock people. `UF_TEST_PROVOKE=vertical.natural_wall_height` reduces the drawn frame to 48px and must fail the geometry check.

Observed 2026-09-19: `codex_naturalwalls_20260919_b` passed 4/4. Opened its sole PNG: separate dark soil and blue-gray stone runs each show a cap above a lower face; the person north of the soil wall is occluded, with people south and to the side visible. Both cap/face halves had 144/144 opaque samples; sprite depth was north 288, wall 336, south 384. `codex_naturalwalls_20260919_negative_b` produced 3 PASS / 1 FAIL with the height provocation: 48x48 frames, 144/0 cap/face samples; its opened sole PNG shows flat one-row strips and the north person visible. Both snapshots included concurrent external GEN3 and cave-flora changes; this evidence establishes the wall fixture, not approval of that generator/catalog work. An earlier `_a` snapshot stopped before the suite on missing underground flora configuration; its sole opened failure PNG showed the Ground camp, not walls. RMMZ editor F5/F8 remains unchecked.

Since the black wall-top convention (AGENTS.md rule 13, 2026-09-21) the upper 48 px of every natural wall frame is a flat near-black cap (`naturalWallSpec.capColor` `#0a0a10`, a 2 px `#121218` top edge; both inside `#08080C..#121218`), drawn as a fill, not art; only the lower 48 px comes from the look's A4 side autotile. The provoked 48 px frame (`vertical.natural_wall_height`) still draws the look's A4 top.

## The ground's column and its cliffs (DEUS-TSK-FABLE-16, 2026-09-24)

Owner directive 2026-09-24, the hard volumetric terrain invariant: every natural cell whose surface S is +1 has solid under it on the ground; at S = +2 the +1 cell and the ground cell are solid; only cave mouths carved into a hill break it. Column profiles (generator 4 and 5 baselines, `surfaceElevation`):

| S | +2 | +1 | Ground (z 0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | floor (ramp) | solid | solid |
| 1 | open | floor or ramp | solid (a carved cave mouth: floor / stairs down) |
| 0 | open | open | floor or ramp |

- **Shapes** were already volumetric (generator 4): `shapeAt`/`shapeCodeAt` answer `solid` for the ground under a hill, and `UF.World.walkable` and the path planner refuse solid and open shapes. Natural ramps are on the lower cell of a one-step rise (a ground ramp is an S = 0 cell beside S = 1).
- **Tiles** (UF_WorldGen, tileset 91): a solid ground cell is the rock face (`peak_rock`, impassable) on layers 0 and 2 with region 250; carved or dug cells inside a hill are bare rock floor (`rock`); ramps are dry ground. See `docs/systems/UF_WorldGen.md`. A ground shape change (a tunnel dug with `setShape`, filled again) repaints the cell and its neighbours with `UF.WorldGen.groundTilesAt`, the same tiles a fresh build paints; cells the start template paints and layers with a saved tile diff are left alone; the changed cell's ground shade (layer 1) is cleared.

## Strata (DEUS-TSK-FABLE-19A, 2026-09-24)

The five-strata model of `docs/DEUS_TSK_FABLE_19_HANDOFF.md` (sections 0, 2, 8, 9): every 5 ft cell of every level is five **strata**, S0 (bottom) to S4 (top). A stratum is 2 ft since WG.00.17 (DEC-013). It was 1 ft when 19A was written: the record is the same, only the feet a stratum stands for changed, and the generator-5 parameters below that say "ft" count strata. A column of the range's levels is levels × 5 strata, elevation `e = (z − zMin) × 5 + s`: 0 at the lowest level's S0; 24 at +2's S4 at the legacy range, 159 at +15's S4 at -16..+15. The strata are the one mutable authority for matter and void. The shape codes the rest of the game reads (`solid`, `floor`, `open`, `ramp`, stairs) are derived from them in one function (`derivePacked`) and nowhere else. 19A adds no cuts, caves, fluids or rendering: every generated world derives exactly the shapes, materials and checksums it had before.

**A stratum** is two bytes: its material (id in the low 6 bits, 0 = air; bit `0x80` = constructed) and its HP (0..255 = 0..100 % of the material's max HP; air and fluids 0).

| id | key | solid | maxHP | support | debris | resist (damage × by type; unlisted 1) |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 0 | air | no | 0 | 0 | - | - |
| 1 | stone | yes | 120 | 1 | rubble | impact 0.5, dig 1, blast 1, fire 0.1 |
| 2 | soil | yes | 40 | 0.5 | loose_earth | impact 1, dig 2, blast 1.5, fire 0.2 |
| 3 | wood | yes | 60 | 0.7 | broken_timber | impact 1, dig 1, blast 1.2, fire 2 |
| 4 | water | fluid | 0 | 0 | - | - |
| 5 | lava | fluid | 0 | 0 | - | - |

The HP, support and resist numbers are diagnostic placeholders, not balanced. `STRATA_MATERIALS` exposes the table.

**Storage** (sparse since WG.00.17, ADR-003 15.3; `docs/systems/DEUS_ZRange.md` §8). A level's seeded baseline keeps its strata in chunks of 32 × 32 cells:
- A **UNIFORM** chunk is one cell code (five material bytes and a connector) from a palette shared by every baseline, with no arrays.
- A **MIXED** chunk holds `m` (5 bytes a cell: cell `li`, stratum `k` at `li × 5 + k`) and `conn` (4 bits a cell: `ramp`, `stairUp`, `stairDown`, `stairBoth`): 5,632 B.
- The directory `dir` (`Uint16Array`, 2 B a chunk; `0xffff` = MIXED, else the palette index) is built when the baseline is made and never saved. The generator works on dense arrays; `seal` converts them and drops them (`toStrata` then `chunkify`).
- Reads go through `storeLocate` and allocate nothing.
- A level outside the core is all UNIFORM (`outerBaseline`): 128 B of directory per 256 × 256 area.

The baseline's HP plane is implicit: an unchanged solid stratum is at full HP by definition, and every change goes into a changed-cell record that carries both bytes of all five strata.

Measured (seed 18, the start area): the core's five levels hold 1,622,552 B of store (directories, MIXED arrays, caps), against 1,802,240 B (1.72 MiB) of the dense arrays before WG.00.17. At -16..+15 the store is 1,693,592 B: 27 more directories and the 12 MIXED chunks of the mountain rock above +2. The cached shape grids (below) come on top.

The biome codes (131,072 B) and the shared surface-height grid (65,536 B) are kept as before. `baseline().strata.m` and `.conn` are dense compat copies built on first read (nothing in the game reads them). `baseline().shape`, `.material` and `.water` are read-only legacy views rebuilt from the strata on first read (checksums, metrics, older tools). `strataMemory(ax, ay)` reports the bytes per level, and `chunkInfo(ax, ay, z)` a level's chunks.

Baseline profiles (what `toStrata` writes): solid → 5 solid strata of the cell's material; floor and stairs → S0 (stairs plus their connector); ramp → S0..S2 plus the connector (the compatibility step); open → 5 air. A natural pool is fluid in S1..S2 over its S0: water on -1, lava on -2.

**Changed cells.** An 11-byte record `[connector, m0..m4, hp0..hp4]`, saved as 22 hex digits in `UF.World.state.levels[z].strata["ax,ay"][cellIndex]` (sparse: a record equal to the baseline is deleted). At run time the records are decoded once into `Map`s keyed by `UF.World.state` identity (rebuilt on a load or a New Game), so a read allocates nothing. A record that can't be read (bad hex, unknown material byte, HP on a non-solid stratum) is skipped with a `console.error` and left in the save untouched.

**The derived shape** (`derivePacked`, per cell, reading the cell and the cells directly above and below):

| Strata of the cell | Derived shape |
|---|---|
| 5 solid strata stacked from S0 (fill 5) | `solid` |
| a connector code and fill < 5 | that connector (`ramp`, stairs) |
| fill 1..4, or fill 0 over a cell whose S4 is solid | `floor` when the standing surface has ≥ 4 non-solid strata of headroom (counted on into the cell above; open sky above the top level `zMax` unless the column is capped), else `solid` (can't be stood in) |
| fill 0 over a non-solid S4 below, or fill 0 on the bottom level `zMin` (nothing below it) | `open` |

**The derived shapes are a compatibility view** (owner review, 2026-09-24): `shapeAt`, `shapeCodeAt`, `standableShape`, `isConnectorCell` and the cached grids answer walkability and the older readers only. They are not authority for vertical sight, cave-roof occlusion, shaft openness or exposure depth: an open cell over a supporting solid stratum reads as a walkable floor, and whether a vertical line of sight passes is a strata question (`hasOpaqueOverburden`, the strata of the cells below). The depth renderer (19C) must ask the strata, never infer "hole or floor" from a shape code.

Material and the constructed flag come from the stratum stood on (the top of the fill, or the S4 below). Consequences: a 1/5 to 4/5 cell is a floor at that height (`surfaceHeightAt`, `heightStateAt`); an open cell over a solid cell reads as a floor on that cell's top (the only place where the derivation differs from the old packed shapes; no generated baseline has it, and the migration counts it).

**Cached shape grids.** The derived codes of a whole level of an area are kept per world state (`Uint8Array(size × size)`, 65,536 B per level; the last 15 grids): built from the strata with `derivePacked` on first use after a load or a New Game, and refreshed by the delta writer (`putDelta`) for the changed cell and the cells above and below it, the only cells whose derivation reads it. They are a read-only view, never saved: a shape read (`shapeAt`, `shapeCodeAt`, `standableShape`, the planner) is one array read, and whole-level passes (painting a level, `shapeGrid`, the flood wall grids, the ground's cliffs and connectors) read the grid instead of deriving 65,536 cells again. `verifyPackedGrids(ax, ay)` re-derives every cell of the built grids and reports differences (diagnostics, tests).

**One writer.** `setStrata(ref, { m, hp, connector }, { constructed, refuseIfStanding, cause })`, `setShape` and the damage API all end in `writeCell`: it stores the record, compares the derived shapes of the cell and of the cells above and below before and after, and for each level whose derived cell changed redraws it (`redrawAround`), emits `levels:shapeChanged` and `levels:cellChanged` (`notifyWorldCellChanged`); a material change of the cell itself also redraws it and emits `levels:cellChanged`; every write emits `levels:strataChanged`; an HP-only change emits `levels:strataChanged` alone. `setShape` keeps its old events and refusals (V68: a unit on the cell and a shape that can't be stood on). A save whose `strataSchemaVersion` this build doesn't know refuses every write.

**Damage.** `applyStrataDamage(area, x, y, z, stratum, damage, damageType = "impact", { source })` hits one stratum; `applyVolumeDamage(area, minX, minY, minZ, minS, maxX, maxY, maxZ, maxS, damage, damageType, { source })` hits every stratum of the box, from level `minZ` stratum `minS` up to level `maxZ` stratum `maxS` on the column's elevation scale (`e = (z − zMin) × 5 + s`, no cap at 24; so a box from Z-1:S4 to Z0:S0 is two strata of one column); `applyVolumeDamage({ center: { area, x, y, z, s }, radius (ft), damage, damageType, falloff: "constant" | "linear" | "quadratic", source })` hits every stratum whose middle (`((x + 0.5) × 5, (y + 0.5) × 5, (e + 0.5) × STRATUM_FEET)` ft; `UF.Space.STRATUM_FEET` is 2 since WG.00.17, so a radius reaches half as many strata up and down as before) is inside the sphere, with damage × falloff(distance / radius); both forms cross levels. Effective damage = damage × the material's resist for the type, then the hooks (`registerDamageResponse(materialKey | "*" | "fluid", fn)`; a solid material's hook may return the effective damage; fluid strata take no HP damage in 19A and only run the fluid hooks). HP bytes lost = ceil(effective × 255 / maxHP); at 0 the stratum becomes air. Each damaged stratum emits `levels:strataDamaged`; a destroyed one also `levels:strataDestroyed` with its debris key (no items are dropped in 19A). A cell is written once per call. No collapse, falling or support propagation in 19A: `effectiveSupport` is a diagnostic number only.

**Adapters** (no allocation; arguments `(ref)`, `(area, x, y[, z])` or `(ax, ay, x, y, z)`): `surfaceHeightAt` (top stratum 0..4 of the solid base from S0, -1 when S0 isn't solid), `worldStrataElevationAt` (the column elevation `e` of the stratum stood on, including the S4 of the cell below; -1 when none), `heightStateAt` (`HEIGHT_k_OF_5`), `fluidStateAt` (`FLUID_k_OF_5`), `isSolid`, `solidFraction`, `dominantMaterial`, `remainingStructuralHP`, `maxStructuralHP`, `effectiveSupport`, `hasOpaqueOverburden` (a solid stratum above the cell's solid base inside the cell, or any solid stratum in the cells above up to the top level: a 5-bit mask per cell; since 19B also the column's ceiling cap; since WG.00.17 the levels above a chunk column's highest possible solid stratum are skipped, `colTops`). `surfaceAt(ref)` bundles them for diagnostics. `UF.Floors.isRoofed` now asks `hasOpaqueOverburden` (exported as `UF.Floors.hasOpaqueOverburden` and `UF.Rooms.hasOpaqueOverburden`) instead of "below the ground, or the cell above can be stood on", then the enclosed-room rule as before; without the strata API it falls back to the old rule. Differences: a cell under a deck two levels up is now roofed; a cell below the ground under a hole is not.

**Fluid adapter** (for `DEUS_Fluid`, which keeps its 0..7 depth scale and is unchanged): `getStrataFluidPassage(…)` → bits: capacity 0..7 (the cell's non-solid strata in fluid depth units, `FLUID_PASS.CAPACITY_MASK`), `DOWN` 8 (S0 open and the cell below's S4 open; never on the bottom level), `UP` 16 (S4 open and the cell above's S0 open; on the top level unless the column is capped, 19B), `SIDE` 32 (any stratum open); 0 for a solid cell. `fluidDepthToStrata(d)` = round(d × 5 / 7) (0..7 → 0,1,1,2,3,4,4,5) and `strataToFluidDepth(k)` = round(k × 7 / 5) (0..5 → 0,1,3,4,6,7). The pools' flood seeding (`isFlooded`) and `isNaturalWaterAt` / `isLavaAt` read the fluid strata (baseline plus changes). Below the bottom level (`zMin`) there is nothing to stand on: an open cell dug through it derives `open`. The owner rule "no floor on -2 is lava" (2026-09-24) is not drawn, flooded or reported by `isLavaAt` yet; it belongs with 19B/19C. The natural lava pools stay on -2 at every range (WG.00.17 keeps the rule; where lava belongs now that levels exist below -2 is an Owner question).

**Old saves.** `migrateSaveToFiveStrata(state)` runs on load (after the pre-V80 migration, before the checksum check) and from `ensureWorldLevels` when `strataSchemaVersion` is missing: each legacy `levels[z].cells[area][i]` packed shape becomes a record (solid → 5 strata of its material at full HP; floor and stairs → S0 plus the connector; ramp → S0..S2 plus the connector; open → 5 air; the constructed flag carried; a natural pool's fluid kept where the new shape leaves air), dropped when it equals the baseline; `levels[z].cells` is removed and `strataSchemaVersion: 1` set; a record `{ rule: "strata", to: 1, converted, droppedAsBaseline, invalid, shapeChanged, normalizedOpen, errors }` goes to `state.migrations` when anything was converted or refused. Entries that can't be read are kept, unapplied, in `levels[z].unmigratedCells` with a `console.error`. A save with an unknown `strataSchemaVersion` is left untouched (`console.error`), its level changes aren't read and every write is refused. New worlds start at schema 1 with `strata: {}`.

**Performance.** Shape reads are one array read of the cached grid; the strata adapters read at most five cells' five bytes; nothing allocates (module-level scratch; the query arguments are parsed without objects). The read count is the path planner's (the busiest sample held about 506,000 reads in both builds); the same seed's `world.path_budget` ran identical searches at 1.22 ms a plan against 6.06 ms before. The Node harness's `query_cost` compares the two codes on the same cells in one process.

## Natural cuts and caves (DEUS-TSK-FABLE-19B / WG.00.08, 2026-09-25)

Generator 5 (the baseline of new worlds) is generator 4 plus meso-scale natural features carved into the strata: ravines, canyons, chasms, fissures, sinkholes, stream cuts, stepped terraces, and cave networks (chambers, passages, branches, dead ends, loops, shafts, mouths, skylights) on the five core levels. Handoff: `docs/handoffs/HANDOFF_DEUS_TSK_FABLE_19B_CUTS_CAVES.md`. **Units (WG.00.17):** the parameters in this section were written when a stratum was 1 ft. The generator is unchanged, so read every "ft" below as strata (2 ft each now). A feature is strata and nothing else: every carve turns solid strata into air through the column (`carveNaturalFeatures`), and the shapes, walkability, overburden, clearance and fluid passage all follow from the strata as before. There is no cave grid or cut mask: the plans are the generator's working data, dropped after the carve.

**Where it runs (pipeline).** `generateBaseline(seed, gen, z, …)` for generator 5 hands out one level of `volumeOf(seed, gen, ax, ay, size)`: the five core levels of the area are generated together (generator 4's `levelArrays` for each level, converted to strata by `toStrata`), then carved in one pass, in this order:
1. host rock and protections (below);
2. cave networks planned on all five levels (+2 first), shafts between networks of adjacent levels planned;
3. the +2 massifs built round the +2 networks (their +2 strata filled with stone and their columns capped, see "The ceiling cap");
4. every network carved (chambers, passages, dead ends, loops, the sloped multi-Z passages, mouths), then the shafts and the skylights;
5. the cuts planned and carved;
6. ramp connectors where a carved floor steps 1 ft up onto a floor of the next level;
7. every solid stratum no longer connected (up, down or sideways) to bedrock (elevation 0) or the area's edge is removed (a 3-D flood from bedrock; a few crust strata a seed, left isolated over a -1 hall between cut cells);
8. the descriptors kept on the ground baseline (`naturalFeatures`).
Since WG.00.17 the core's five baselines are then sealed into chunk stores, and the levels of the range outside the core are added (`outerBaseline`: rock below, air above), with the caps' rock materialized above +2 ("The ceiling cap"). The carve itself works in the core frame (elevation 0..24, index z + 2), frozen with generator 5.
It runs when the area's baselines are first generated (`world:initializing` at New Game, or on demand), before factions, history, objects and the map builds read the terrain; everything later reads the final strata. The result is a pure function of the seed, the generator version, the area and the world's size and catalog climate: the water model is asked with a stand-in state of the seed (`WorldGen.waterModel({ seed, size, areasX, areasY, startArea })`), the biome family from `WorldGen.fieldsFor` with the start's climate blending, never the live `UF.World.state`, so another seed's baseline built inside a live world (checksums) is the same as in its own. Iteration order is fixed; nothing depends on frames. The last 3 areas' volumes are kept (a dropped one regenerates identically).

**Parameters** are frozen per generator in `FEATURE_PARAMS[5]` (exported): a change is a new generator version, never an edit (a save regenerates its baselines from them).

**Protections.** Nothing is carved within 12 cells of an area edge (full depth from 20: areas regenerate independently) or within 40 cells of each area's centre (the founders' flat valley is r 30, blended by r 36; full depth from r 48). Cuts and caves stay 3 cells clear of the cliff cave mouths, their tunnels, the -1 stair landings and the corridors they dig (generator 4 now records each mouth's `corridor`); caves stay out of the underground founding squares and pools (radius + 3), and a cut over them stops above the pocket's roof (the next level's S0). No cut, skylight or mouth comes within 2 cells of surface water (the valley floor's ocean, lakes, rivers and pond). Massif cells take no cut.

**Cuts.** Anchors on a 36 × 36 lattice of the area, one seeded try per lattice cell, chance 0.5 (about 13 cuts an area). Each takes a depth class, then a type by the biome family of its anchor, then its shape:
- **Classes** (depth below the anchor's rock top, and the lowest first-air elevation it may carve to): shallow 60 % (1–4 ft, floor ≥ 7), medium 25 % (5–8 ft, floor ≥ 7), deep 12 % (9–13 ft, floor ≥ 6: the whole -1 band exposed), z2 3 % (bed at 1–3 ft: reaches -2; -2 S0 is never carved, below it is only lava). The valley floor stands on the ground's S0 (first air 11 ft), 1 ft above -1: a valley cut deeper than 1 ft always opens -1's upper strata, so shallow and medium cuts keep -1's lower strata and only the deep and z2 classes go further.
- **Families** (geological tendencies, not rules) from the climate fields blended toward the start as the biome map is: VOLC volcanism > 0.62; HIGH a +2 summit or elevation > mountainLevel − 0.08; WET rainfall > 0.58 and drainage < 0.45; ARID rainfall < 0.28 (or hot and < 0.36); else TEMP. Types: TEMP ravine, karst sinkhole, stream cut, limestone cleft (arroyo, fissure rarely); WET drainage cut, peat hollow, wet sinkhole, water channel; ARID arroyo, canyon, slot chasm, terrace steps; HIGH fault chasm, granite cleft, rock cut, scree terrace; VOLC fissure, tube collapse, caldera fracture, basalt steps. Only the types marked deep take the deep and z2 classes.
- **Shapes.** Lines (a centre line walked both ways from the anchor with a noise-bent heading; half-width varying along it and tapering at the tips), arcs (caldera fractures), round pits with an irregular rim (sinkholes, hollows), chains of pits over a shallow trough (tube collapse). The bed is deepest in the middle and rises toward the tips, with ±1 ft of noise; a line crossing lower ground simply ends there (a cell is cut only where the floor is below its own rock top). Cross-sections: vertical walls with a 1 ft lip (clefts, fissures, chasms), U (ravines, channels), flat bed with banks (arroyos, stream and drainage cuts), steep, stepped ledges of 2–3 ft (canyons, terraces), funnel, bowl and throat (sinkholes, hollows, collapse pits). The floor of each cell is measured from its own rock top, so shoulders, banks, ledges, lips and rims fall at partial heights: HEIGHT_1_OF_5 … HEIGHT_5_OF_5 floors all occur.
- **Carve.** Every stratum from the cut's floor up to the rock's top becomes air (a column holding a fluid stratum there is left alone), the connectors of the levels it reaches are dropped. Near the edges and flat centres the depth fades with the protection weight.

**Caves.** One candidate per 64 × 64 lattice cell and level, up to 8 seeded tries in the cell for host rock; per level (chance per candidate, first-air floor of its chambers, clearance of chambers and passages): +2 0.3 (21 ft; chambers 20–21 ft; 4–6 / 4 ft), +1 0.35 (15–16 ft, under a +2 summit; 4–5 / 4), ground 0.3 (10–11 ft, under a hill; 5–8 / 4–5), -1 0.22 (5–6 ft; 5–7 / 4–5), -2 0.2 (1–2 ft; 5–7 / 4–5). About 10 networks an area, 2–3 % of its columns. A network is 2–6 chambers (ellipses of radius 2.5–5.5 with an irregular rim and raised floor patches a stratum up) 7–13 cells apart, joined by passages (half-width 0.7–1.3, slightly bent), with branches (35 %), 0–2 dead ends (short passages to an alcove) and sometimes a loop (20 %).
- **The void.** A cell's void is strata F … C − 1 with C = min(F + clearance, the rock's top − 1): at least 1 ft of rock stays above (a capped column may be carved to the top of +2: the cap is its roof). The floor stratum F − 1 must be solid; the carve stops a stratum short of an existing void above (a 1 ft slab between a new cave and a generator-4 hall), and skips a cell whose void would be under 3 ft, holds a fluid, or is protected. Voids span levels: a ground cave under a +2 summit is up to 8 ft high through the ground cell and +1's lower strata.
- **Multi-Z** (rare): a network's last chamber one level down (or up, from -1), the passage to it sloping at most 1 ft a cell (25 % of the networks that can); two networks on adjacent levels joined by a shaft (40 % of pairs with chambers within 20 cells: the lower network reaches under the upper one's chamber, and a disc of radius 1.2–1.8 opens the rock between). About 1.5 multi-Z networks an area.
- **Mouths** (60 % of networks on the ground and above): a passage from the nearest chamber out to open ground whose surface is at the network's floor (a valley cell for the ground's caves, a +1 terrace for +1's, the open summit outside the massif for +2's), its floor sloping to the exit's height. **Skylights** (25 %, below +2): a shaft from a chamber up to the open sky through at most 10 ft of rock (a natural sinkhole into a cave; from a -2 cave under the valley, one open column from the sky to -2).
- **Fluids.** No void, shaft or skylight carves through a fluid: a column holding a fluid stratum anywhere in the interval it would open is left as it is (checked before anything is written), and only solid strata become air (corrected 2026-09-25 after the Grok review ATK-19B-002: shafts turned fluid into air and skylights stopped at a fluid only after carving the rock below it). Generated worlds keep pools out of their paths anyway (the founding-square locks), so no generated world changed.

**Ramps.** A carved cell whose floor steps exactly 1 ft up onto a floor of the next level gets the ramp connector on its own level (derived shape `ramp`; the strata keep the partial height). Generator 4's natural ramps are unchanged.

**Clearance** (physical data only; no creature's needs are decided here):
| Query | Returns |
|---|---|
| `continuousAirHeight(ref \| area, x, y[, z] \| ax, ay, x, y, z)` | Strata of continuous **air** (× `UF.Space.STRATUM_FEET` for feet; the count was also the feet before WG.00.17) above the cell's standing surface (the stratum `worldStrataElevationAt` reports), counted up through the cells above to the first stratum that isn't air, solid **or fluid**: 1 solid + 4 air under solid = 4; 5 air on the S4 below under solid = 5; a void across -1 and the ground = 6 and more; stone + 4 water = 0; stone + 2 air + 2 water = 2; -2 stone + 4 lava = 0 (water and lava are not clearance, and the air above a fluid stratum is not counted from the floor). `Infinity` when every stratum above up to the top level's S4 is air and the column has no cap (open sky); 0 for a solid cell; -1 when there is nothing to stand on. No allocation. (Corrected 2026-09-25 after the Grok review ATK-19B-001: it counted fluid strata as open volume.) The derived shapes keep their own headroom rule (a pool floor, fill 1 with water in S1..S2, is still a `floor`) |
| `airRunAt(area, x, y, e)` or `(ax, ay, x, y, e)` | The same from column elevation e (`(z − zMin) × 5 + s`; 0..24 at the legacy range) upward, ending at the first solid or fluid stratum: 0 when e itself is solid or fluid |
`cellAt` is unchanged (WorldGen's underground pass calls it for every cell of a level build: ask the clearance queries directly). The derived shapes are unchanged (a 4 ft space reads `floor` in the compatibility view, a 3 ft one `solid`): walkability for a creature is for later systems to decide from the clearance.

**The ceiling cap.** The generator's model ends at +2 (its core). A +2 cave needs rock above it, so a +2 network sits in a **massif**: the summit cells within 3 of the network (only S = 2 cells, only unprotected ones) have their +2 strata S1..S4 filled with stone (the one place the generator adds matter: the mountain rising above the model), and each such column gets a **cap**, the minimal record of the rock above +2:
- representation: `baseline(2).caps`, a `Map` cell → `material | thickness << 8` (material byte: stone; thickness 3–12 strata, seeded), at full HP; a changed cap is saved sparse in `UF.World.state.levels[zMax].caps["ax,ay"][cell]` (`levels["2"]` at the legacy range) = 6 hex digits (material, thickness, HP 0..255), `"000000"` = breached; a record equal to the baseline is deleted. Read allocation-free through `capCode`;
- material and thickness: `capAt(ref)` → `{ material, thickness, hp, maxHP, remainingHP, opaque, support, anchored, changed }` or null;
- opacity and exposure: `hasOpaqueOverburden` is true under a cap; `continuousAirHeight` ends at it; a +2 floor's headroom ends at it (`derivePacked`: without a cap the sky above +2 is unlimited headroom); `getStrataFluidPassage` gives no UP at +2 under a cap;
- HP and damage: `applyCapDamage(ref, damage, damageType, { source })`: one body of material maxHP × thickness points, the material's resist and damage hooks as a stratum's; at 0 HP the cap is breached (removed): `levels:capBreached({ area, x, y, material, thickness, debris, damageType, source })`, and the column is open to the sky; `setCap(ref, spec | null)` sets or removes one. Every cap change emits `levels:capChanged(ref, { before, after, cause })`, refreshes +2's derived cell and redraws it when its shape changed;
- roof and support: the cap is anchored rock (the mountain continues above the model): `support` is its material's, `anchored` true. No support or collapse simulation (out of 19B's scope).
- WG.00.17: at a range taller than +2 the rock of a cap is strata of +3 upward (`materializeCaps`: a cap of t strata fills S0.. of +3 upward, five a level). What doesn't fit under `zMax` stays a cap above the top level, held by the top level's baseline (`topCaps`). At -16..+15 every cap fits; at -2..+2 every cap stays a cap, as before. The rock is the same matter either way (5,160 strata for seed 18).

A cap is not a level: it has no cells, shapes, units or map.

**Descriptors.** `naturalFeatures(ax = 0, ay = 0)` → a copy of `{ gen, cuts: [{ id, type, family, depthClass, anchor, hostTop, bed, form, profile, cells, minFloor, maxDepth, floorLevel, hostLevel, deepest, length | pits }], caves: [{ id, level, floor, chambers, passages, deadEnds, loops, multiZ, levels, nodes, cells, maxClearance, massifCells, mouth, skylight, joins }], shafts, skylights, placement, massifCells, capCells, carvedStrata, cutCells, caveCells, rampsAdded, floatingRemoved, floatingAt, ms }` (null below generator 5): what the generator did, for diagnostics, tests and the report. It is never read as terrain.

**Saves and old worlds.** A save keeps each level's generator version: every world made before 19B stays generator 4 and regenerates byte for byte as before (checked against the pre-19B code). A pre-V80 save now migrates to generator 4 explicitly (`PRE_CUT_GEN`), so no cut opens under a settlement that predates the levels; a level entry missing from a save takes the generator of the levels it has (a column is one generator). A New Game may name an older generator with `UF.NewGameSetup.levelsGen` (4 or later; tests pinning generator 4). The checksum of a generator-5 level is FNV-1a over its strata bytes, connectors, biome codes and caps (the ground's: the WorldGen lattice then its strata), since partial fills and caves don't show in the legacy codes. Save impact: none for untouched terrain (baselines regenerate); a changed cap adds one 6-digit record.

**Known limits (19B).**
- Seen in the running game (native Playtest-mode runs on a snapshot, seed 18, 2026-09-25): the features are physically there (the queried cells match the harness) but hard to read: the ground's open cells draw as rock face beside the hills' rock face, and -1/-2 are dominated by the pre-existing blue flood overlay. Making cuts and caves legible is 19C's (the depth compositor).
- The ground's open cells (a cut down to -1 or -2) are painted as the impassable rock face (`peak_rock`, region 250) until the depth compositor (19C) shows what lies below; the -1/-2 cut floors draw with the underground biome's floor look and get the underground flora and minerals (WorldGen's underground pass), not the surface biome's.
- Cuts are dry: stream cuts, drainage cuts and water channels are erosional forms without water; no water table, aquifer, waterfall or rapids (out of 19B's scope). The existing flood model (`isFlooded`) floods -1/-2 cells from the ground's water as before; on seed 20260923 generator 5 has 2.0 % more flooded cells at -1 (34,440 against 33,765) and 0.7 % more at -2 (31,243 against 31,027) than generator 4: the pre-existing model already floods about half of each underground level, rock included.
- Level transitions for units are not implemented (slice 2): the ramp connectors are what a future planner will use; today a unit stays on its level's floor cells.
- The z2 class and the -2 skylights are the only features reaching -2: 9 of seeds 1–24 have a cut reaching -2 (every seed of the regression set 18, 3, 21, 4 does). A deterministic regression seed proves each class (the handoff's rule), not every world.
- Multi-area worlds: a feature never crosses an area edge (12-cell margin), and `cellInfo(z = 0).walkable` asks the ground's shapes, which builds another area's baselines when that area is asked about.

## The view

- **Keys:** `,` (also `<`) up, `.` (also `>`) down, Home the ground. A press in a frame when the map can't switch (a transfer finishing, an event running) is kept for 30 frames.
- **Level plate:** top right, left of the speed buttons (UF_TimeSpeed): `▼  -1  ▲`; the arrows are buttons; drawn in code (AR-1312 will replace it). The labels are `+z`, `Ground` and `-z` (`+15` … `-16` at the default range); an arrow is disabled at its end of the Z range.
- A switch is an RMMZ transfer with no fade to the level's map; the cursor cell and the display position are put back; the zoom stays. It doesn't autosave and doesn't clear the image cache (it's a camera move, not a journey; VERTICAL_BUILD_PLAN §12 D-3).
- **Follow mode:** when `$colonyManager.cameraFollowUnit` names a unit on another level of the area on screen, the view switches to that level centred on it.
- Units, items and objects of other levels are not drawn and not clickable: their events don't exist on this level's map, and the object and item layers draw the level on screen.

## Save data (`UF.World.state`, version 4)
- `zRange = { zMin, zMax }` (WG.00.17): the world's levels; absent in a save made before WG.00.17, which keeps -2..+2 (`docs/systems/DEUS_ZRange.md` §9).
- `levels["<z>"] = { z, gen, checksum, strata: { "ax,ay": { cellIndex: "22 hex digits" } } }`: the core levels -2..+2 always, a level outside the core only while it has a change (no checksum) (strata schema 1, section Strata); `strataSchemaVersion: 1` on the state. Saves made before the strata had `cells: { "ax,ay": { cellIndex: packed } }`, `packed = shape | constructed × 8 | material × 16`; `migrateSaveToFiveStrata` converts them on load. `checksumMismatch: true` is added on load when a regenerated baseline differs (reported by a console warning, never repaired).
- `levels["<zMax>"].caps = { "ax,ay": { cellIndex: "6 hex digits" } }` (19B; `levels["2"]` at the legacy range): a changed ceiling cap (material, thickness, HP; `"000000"` = breached), sparse; absent when every cap is as generated.
- `view = { x, y, z }`: the level on screen and the cursor cell.
- `version: 4`; `migrations: [{ from, to: 4, rule: "V80", counts: { units, items, jobs, regrow }, conflicts: [] }]` after a pre-V80 load.
- Records: `unit.z`, `unit.goal.z`, `item.z`, `job.target.z`, `job.stand.z`, regrow `z`. Level objects are in `objectDiffs["ax,ay,z"]`; ground keys are unchanged.
- **Migration** (on load when `version < 4` or `levels` is missing): writes `z: 0` on every unit (and goal), item, job target and stand and regrow entry, adds the five core level entries (the four new levels regenerated from the seed), `view` on the ground, `version: 4` and one migration record. Nothing moves; no id, diff, object diff or fog key changes.

## Known problems
- Strata (DEUS-TSK-FABLE-19A): no collapse, falling or support propagation; a unit can be left standing over a stratum that damage removed (only `setShape`/`setStrata` with `refuseIfStanding` refuse). Destroyed strata drop no items (the debris key is reported only). Material HP, support and resist values are diagnostic placeholders.
- Strata: `setShape(ref, "open")` over a solid cell now reads as a floor on that cell's top (the strata rule); the `depth` suite's terrace-hole fixture (a +1 terrace cell set open over solid ground) therefore shows a floor, not a hole, while its checks still pass. A real hole needs the cell below opened too.
- Strata: the owner rule "no floor on -2 is lava" (2026-09-24) is only in the derivation (at the legacy range nothing is below -2 to stand on; at taller ranges -2 stands on -3's rock); lava is not drawn, flooded or reported there. 19B/19C.
- Strata: `surfaceHeightAt` returns the stratum index (0..4, -1 when S0 isn't solid) and `worldStrataElevationAt` the column elevation (`(z − zMin) × 5 + s`): two allocation-free calls instead of the handoff's one call returning both (`surfaceAt` returns both in one object, allocating).
- Found while checking 19A (the pre-strata build too: seed 20260923 in both, 23 animals in each, the same first four listed): walking wildlife stands on ground cells inside hills (derived and legacy shape `solid`): deer, wild sheep, hares, wolves, arctic foxes, a troll. A Wildlife placement or roaming matter, not the strata.
- Every level's map must simulate concurrently regardless of the view. Combat now groups living units by level; unit movement and jobs also advance off view. Remaining `currentArea()`-gated wildlife decisions are an integration gap, not an acceptable final view-based simulation pause. Speech bubbles, stance squares and action overlays may remain view-filtered because they only render.
- Units don't change level in play yet (slice 2); `take` keeps colonists on their own level.
- UF_Fog stores a level's fog under `map:<id>` (fog is off for development); the plan's `area:x,y,z` key is a one-line change in UF_Fog.
- Right-click options (UF_Interact) are ground-only: on another level nothing is offered.
- Generator-2 pools use existing stock A1 freshwater; liquids do not flow and no aquifer pressure is simulated.
- The 2026-09-19 `vertical_b` runtime reported open-air passage on +1: UF_Tiles applied a passable Ground shade overlay to non-Ground maps, masking the blocking base tile. The Ground-only shade guard is owned by UF_Tiles; its runtime retest is pending. `switch_view` logs actual shape, tileset, tile IDs/flags and bridge state without relaxing blocked-air assertions. Its underground countercell is explicitly emptied/restored so natural resources cannot be mistaken for the Ground fixture leaking across levels.
