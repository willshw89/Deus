## 1. Executive Summary & Frozen Principle

$$\textbf{Ordinary competent work succeeds because physical requirements are satisfied.}$$
$$\textbf{Skills mostly determine how well, how quickly, and with how much waste—not whether somebody randomly forgets how to build a wall.}$$

In Project DEUS, humanoids and colonists are competent creatures of the simulation. A worker does not roll a d20 every time they swing an axe, hammer a nail, roast a flank of venison, or carry a log. 

### The Frozen Principle:
> **DEUS uses SRD ability checks where uncertainty is meaningful. Routine physical work succeeds deterministically when its material, tool, workstation, knowledge, and time requirements are satisfied. Skill and proficiency primarily create upside—speed, efficiency, quality, durability, safety, and exceptional results. Failure penalties are concentrated in genuinely difficult, contested, rushed, hazardous, or technically advanced tasks rather than ordinary life.**

## 2. The Four Action Classes

Every physical or social action executed in Project DEUS belongs to one of four discrete action classes:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        DEUS FOUR ACTION CLASSES                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. ROUTINE:                                                            │
│    - Examples: Build wooden wall, chop timber, dig dirt, roast basic   │
│      meat, haul stone, tailor simple burlap wrap.                      │
│    - Check Behavior: NO FAILURE ROLL. Deterministic 100% success.      │
│    - Role of Skill: Modifies work rate (speed) and material scrap.     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. SKILLED:                                                            │
│    - Examples: Forge iron sword, assemble crossbow, sew leather armor, │
│      dress granite ashlar, craft oak barrel, brew herbal poultice.     │
│    - Check Behavior: Deterministic success if competence threshold met.│
│    - Role of Skill: Determines output quality (Standard / Fine),       │
│      durability, material efficiency, and speed.                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. DIFFICULT:                                                          │
│    - Examples: Forge masterwork blade, engineer 5-tile chasm bridge,   │
│      delicate structural shoring, decipher ancient runic inscription.  │
│    - Check Behavior: Meaningful SRD Check (d20 + Ability + Prof).      │
│    - Non-Punitive RNG: Poor roll yields standard/ordinary result or   │
│      takes extra time; high roll yields exceptional masterwork bonuses.│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. CONTESTED / HAZARDOUS:                                              │
│    - Examples: Stabilize dying ally in battle, grapple enemy, pick lock│

│    - Check Behavior: Standard SRD 5.1 d20 Check with REAL DOWNSIDE.    │
│    - Failure Consequence: Legitimate physical harm (falling, crushing, │
│      bleeding out, burning, toxic inhalation).                         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. The Physical Production Formula

For all Routine and Skilled production, work follows an immutable physical law:

$$\mathbf{Materials + Correct\ Tool + Valid\ Workstation + Enough\ Time = Successful\ Output}$$

```text
  [PHYSICAL PREREQUISITES]
  - Physical materials present at site (e.g. 4 Oak Logs)
  - Appropriate tool equipped or on site (e.g. Wood Axe, Hammer)
  - Authorized workstation (e.g. Carpentry Bench or building parcel)
  - Sufficient labor ticks allocated
                 │
                 ▼
  [DETERMINISTIC SUCCESS]
  Output object is guaranteed to emerge upon 100% labor completion.
  No worker ever rolls a 1 and causes an anvil, bed, or stone wall to explode.
```

## 5. The Seven Positive Production Dimensions

Proficiency and skill level primarily operate as **multipliers of positive performance**, expanding value across seven dimensions:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   POSITIVE PRODUCTION DIMENSIONS                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. WORK RATE (Speed):           Labor ticks required per unit of work. │
│ 2. MATERIAL EFFICIENCY:         Reduction of scrap, sawdust, and waste.│
│ 3. OUTPUT QUALITY:              Elevates quality tier: Standard -> Fine│
│ 4. STRUCTURAL DURABILITY:       Bonus HP, impact and wear resistance.  │
│ 5. ENGINEERING PRECISION:       Tighter joints, better weatherproofing.│
│ 6. OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY:         Reduced chance of cuts, strains, burns.│
│ 7. SPECIAL / MASTERWORK RESULT: Unlocks unique cultural/runic traits.  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Proficiency Tier Scaling Multipliers (Illustrative Baseline):
| Competence Tier | Effective Work Rate | Material Waste | Output Quality Floor | Advanced Recipe Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Untrained / Novice** | $1.0\times$ (Baseline) | $+20\%$ scrap | `STANDARD` | Basic survival only |
| **Proficient (Lvl 1–4)** | $1.15\dots 1.30\times$ | Normal (0% scrap) | `STANDARD` (Chance of `FINE`) | Standard civic crafting |
| **Expert (Lvl 5–8)** | $1.30\dots 1.60\times$ | $-10\%$ material | `FINE` (Guaranteed) | Advanced architecture |
| **Master (Lvl 9+)** | $1.60\dots 2.00\times$ | $-20\%$ material | `FINE` (Chance of `MASTERWORK`) | Masterwork / Ancestral |

## 6. The Four Quality Tiers

Every crafted item and constructed architectural piece possesses a discrete quality tier:

```text
  [CRUDE TIER]
  - Origin: Emergency rushed labor, improvised tools, poor materials, or battle damage.
  - Stats: -20% HP, rough appearance, minor negative mood thought if furnished.
  - Note: NEVER produced simply because of a poor dice roll during ordinary work!

  [STANDARD TIER (The Universal Baseline)]
  - Origin: Normal competent construction by novice or proficient workers.
  - Stats: 100% baseline HP, clean functional design, fully serves its purpose.

  [FINE TIER]
  - Origin: Expert craftsmanship, seasoned timber, dressed stone ashlar.
  - Stats: +25% HP, +15% weather/fire resistance, positive settler mood thought.

  [MASTERWORK TIER]
  - Origin: Master artisan + difficult craftsmanship milestone check.
  - Stats: +50% HP, distinctive aesthetic crest, +1 damage/armor rating, renown entry.
```

## 8. The Four Distinct Inputs (Matter vs. Mind)

$$\textbf{Skill does NOT substitute for physical matter.}$$

Every production job strictly separates its requirements into four independent categories:
1. **KNOWLEDGE / SKILL:** Does the worker meet the required competence threshold?
2. **TOOLS:** Does the worker possess or have access to the correct implement (e.g. Iron Hammer, Crosscut Saw, Blacksmith Tongs)?
3. **WORKSTATION:** Is the physical workstation present, functional, and unblocked (e.g. Charcoal Forge, Masonry Trestle, Carpenter's Bench)?
4. **MATERIALS:** Are the physical resources delivered and staged on site (e.g. Hewn Pine Logs, Granite Ashlar, Iron Ingots)?

If any one of these four inputs is missing, the job cannot begin. A master smith with 99 Smithing cannot forge a horseshoe out of thin air.

## 9. Meaningful Checks & Non-Punitive RNG

$$\textbf{Roll at decision points, not during routine labor.}$$

- **Zero Routine Checks:** A 3-day house construction involves zero d20 rolls during hauling or hammering.
- **Milestone Quality Resolution:** At the moment of 100% completion on complex projects, the Project Lead resolves a single **Craftsmanship Resolution Roll**:
  $$\text{Check Total} = \text{d}20 + \text{Lead Ability Mod} + \text{Lead Proficiency Bonus} + \text{Tool Bonus}$$
  - $\text{Total} < \text{DC}$: Result is **`STANDARD`** (Clean, solid, fully functional).
  - $\text{Total} \ge \text{DC}$: Result is **`FINE`**.
  - $\text{Total} \ge \text{DC} + 10$ or **Natural 20**: Result is **`MASTERWORK`**.
- **Natural 1 Handling:** A Natural 1 during routine or skilled crafting simply yields a **Standard baseline result** with zero bonus efficiency. The artisan does **not** destroy materials or hammer their own hand.

## 11. Autonomous AI Labor Dispatch & Urgency Logic

Colony AI (`DEUS_Jobs.js`, `DEUS_Colonists.js`, `DEUS_Projects.js`) uses a 3-tier dispatch heuristic:

```text
  [NEW JOB POSTED]
  1. Competence Filter: Which living colonists meet the required threshold?
  2. Efficiency Ranking: Who possesses the highest relevant Ability + Proficiency?
  3. Urgency Evaluation:
     - NORMAL PACING: Assign the highest-skilled available specialist.
     - EMERGENCY CRISIS (Fire, breach, dying colonist): Bypass skill ranking;
       dispatch the NEAREST conscious colonist immediately. An untrained settler
       with a water bucket can douse a burning wall; an untrained settler can
       apply pressure to an arterial wound to stop immediate death.
```

## 12. Learning Through Use & Historical Parity

1. **Active Simulation Progression:**
   - Performing work awards incremental experience to relevant skills and tool proficiencies.
   - Growth is paced so an initial founder evolves from a competent novice into a revered master craftsman over 10–20 simulation years.
   - Genetics and biological traits are inherited by offspring; **skills and proficiencies are NOT inherited** (they are learned through apprenticeship and education).
2. **Historical Simulation Parity:**
   - The 500-year coarse historical simulation reads the exact same proficiency and trade traditions to determine settlement wealth, architectural quality, and relic production, ensuring macro history and micro gameplay share identical truth.
