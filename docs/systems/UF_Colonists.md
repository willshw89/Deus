# UF_Colonists

## Household and personal-planner integration (2026-09-19)

The user's explicit transfer authorizes the family/housing/planning hooks in this file; other agents' Jobs, Wildlife, Doors, Sheet and catalog implementations are not replaced.

- `effectivePlan(unit)` combines the saved settlement bootstrap with `Households.planSteps(unit)` and that person's `Goals.planSteps(unit)`. Each demand stream has a separate three-step unfinished window. Viable candidates are scored using canonical Skills levels, then CultureGrowth policies, then personal Goals priorities. All execution remains real Jobs gathering, hauling, construction and crafting; no free material or timer-only completion.
- Household steps use `exact:true`: the correct object must occupy every intended cell. Blocked terrain/another building is unfinished, and another home's bed does not count. `params.household`, `goalOwner`, and `goalId` survive into jobs. Personal `each` steps are satisfied only by their named owner's inventory/equipment.
- Supply scans protect all reserved home build cells on the level; buildings, doors and beds are not autonomous raw-material sources. Existing home hearths and assigned beds are preferred by their residents. A resident's own home is exempt from the original camp-distance leash.
- Actual talk completions maintain at most 16 reciprocal social bonds per person. Three conversations can form a household partnership only when both adults affirm a seeded family desire and Households accepts species/site/level/kinship/existing-partner checks. This is a simple relationship foundation, not dating, courtship choices, infidelity, disputed public parentage or marriage law.
- Adult intimacy requires a finite age of at least 18, compatible species, an established or compatible opposite-sex partner, acceptable immediate needs, and not having mated today. While private enclosed bedrooms provide a privacy/comfort mood bonus (+15, "Made love in private room"), couples in open camps, hearths, or unbuilt settlements are no longer sterile: they can mate at their shared home, bed, campfire, or adjacent grounds (+12, "Made love with partner", -50 social need). Mating rolls for pregnancy conception (60% baseline chance or forced).
- `stepFactionReproduction()`: Autonomous periodic reproduction tick evaluating all living factions worldwide. Compatible adult men and women at the same site/settlement pair up, mate, and roll conception without requiring manual orders or luxury bedrooms.
- Birth requires a free standable neighboring cell (searching Chebyshev distance 1 then 2) and a successfully created unit. A newborn unit inherits parents, site, species, and faction identity, receives `$Baby` sprite at age 0, stage `"baby"`, and has a personal life Destiny generated via `UF.Goals.ensure(child)`. Upon birth, `UF.Factions.get(factionId).population` dynamically increments by 1, and `colonists:born` and `factions:born` events are emitted.
- Generational aging: `progressAging()` iterates all faction people worldwide (`allFactionPeople()`). Every 7 days, age increments by 1. Stage and appearance progress through baby (<2, `$Baby`), child (<12, `$Child_Boy`/`$Child_Girl`), teen (<18, `$Teen_Boy`/`$Teen_Girl`), to adult ($\ge 18$, adult tier sprite). At age 18, units attain full adulthood and can court, pair, and reproduce, enabling generational turnover and perpetual population growth.
- `describe(unit)` additionally exposes `household` and structured `lifeGoals`. F7's selected-creature panel is supplied by UF_Goals; no action labels are added over heads.

Focused actual-source checks live in `tools/test_family_integration.js`; the snapshot-only `tools/fixtures/UF_SocietyRuntime.js` exercises real construction/Jobs. Its arenas and material supply are explicitly prepared test setup, not proof of natural terrain abundance or excavation. Editor F5/F8 acceptance remains separate.

## Individual sleep preferences (2026-09-19)

`sleepSchedule(unit)` creates/returns the person's saved `{version:1,bedMinute,wakeMinute,durationMinutes,chronotype}` on `unit.data`. Seed/identity and discipline set quarter-hour bedtime variation; adults prefer 7-9 hours, developing people 9-11. `sleepWindow`, `sleepingHours` and `sleepFrames` expose the actual minute-aware planner timing. Existing valid schedules are retained through save/load; old saves initialize lazily. Moderate fatigue follows the personal window; exhaustion outside it requests a shorter recovery sleep. Hunger/thirst and danger retain their other guards. The ownership-bed scheduler uses the same duration. Bedtime does not automatically cancel explicit orders or force a rested person to sleep.

## Five-level settlement integration (2026-09-19)

The following contract supersedes the single-home scope in the historical description below. Every inhabited, non-ruined founding site has a separate saved settlement plan. The player's people remain `kind: "colonist"`, `ai: "colonist"`; other factions keep `kind: "person"` and their faction identity, with `ai: "settlement"`. An internal settler loop runs needs, decisions, job callbacks, pregnancy and aging for both groups across all five levels. Changing the viewed map does not gate that loop or stockpile adoption. NPCs do not take the player's open designations and cannot receive `Colonists.order` commands.

- Persisted coordinates keep `z` beside `area`: units, `data.home`, jobs, targets and haul destinations. Object/Jobs/Items area APIs receive `{x,y,z}`. Missing z means legacy Ground; invalid explicit z is rejected rather than rounded or silently redirected to Ground. Nonzero work is refused on a World core without the documented level seam.
- `unit.data.site` is the canonical History site ID; `data.home = {area:{x,y},x,y,z}`. A dwarf faction's two underground settlements share faction identity but have separate site IDs, homes, hearth searches, build plans, larders, reservations and progress. A unit temporarily on another level cannot use its old home's coordinates as a home on its new level; cross-level routing is not implemented here.
- `state.colony` retains the legacy primary player-home fields and gains `z`, `settlements: { [siteId]: settlement }` for other sites, and `settlementsReady`. Each settlement has its own `factionId`, `siteId`, `site`, `area`, `z`, `radius`, `plan`, `stockpiles`, `log` and `adopted`. The primary is not repeated in the map, so serialization has no cycle. Culture and plan priorities use the resident's faction.
- Existing saves get missing settlement records/AI on the first enabled scan, preserving the primary plan and existing unit locations, inventory, needs, skills and work rate. This does not relocate saved surface dwarves or regenerate their faction. New Game converts each site's existing founders; it does not duplicate the two-settlement founder budget. Only the pre-existing primary-site minimum-two fallback remains.
- `state(unitOrId?)`, `site(unitOrId?)`, `planStatus(unitOrId?)`, `planText(unitOrId?)`, `stockpiles(unitOrId?)` select that unit's own settlement; omitted arguments preserve the primary-home API. `site` includes `z`. `settlements()` lists primary and additional settlement records. `culture(unitOrSettlement?)` selects that faction's culture. `list()`, `get()`, `isColonist()` and `order()` remain player-only UI/control APIs.
- Water, food, prey, beds, trees, objects, inventory transfers and open jobs are queried on the worker's level. Resource/build/bed reservations do not collide merely because another level has the same x/y. Recipe reservations also distinguish site IDs. Social and mating partners must share level and faction. Births retain the mother's level, site, home and player/NPC identity.
- `order(unitId,{type,target?:{area?,x,y,z?},params?})` defaults an omitted target level to the unit's own level. Explicit invalid or cross-level targets and haul destinations are refused before the current job is cancelled. No level is inferred from the viewed map.
- Non-ground plan cells with unwalkable terrain are skipped: this system does not excavate solid rock or invent structural support over open air. Ground plan behavior, decision intervals, need rates, work rates and personality formulas are otherwise unchanged.

Validation so far: syntax and diff checks; an ad-hoc Node VM loading the actual Colonists and Jobs sources passed 30 contracts, including completion of drink jobs on all five levels while the view changes, separate same-faction settlement plans, player/NPC control separation, births and actual save serialization. A real source mutation dropping a newborn's z failed the birth assertion. These are engine-double checks, not F5 or full-game acceptance; coordinated runtime snapshots remain pending. The original embedded `colonists` suite is retained and still assumes a centered primary ground home in parts of its fixture.

## Historical ground-only description and evidence

The colonists are the people of the player's faction at its home site (the site UF_History placed at the map centre): on `world:created` every `person` unit of that faction at that site becomes a colonist with a name, gender, seeded facets and skills, needs, an empty pack and (humans) clothing tier sheets. Each colonist decides its own work when idle: needs first (drink, eat or forage/hunt, sleep, talk, a walk), then open designations, then the society plan from the catalog (build, craft, stock steps; the culture picks the plan variant, the wall piece and the priorities), else exploring and strolling. Every job is a UF_Jobs job, so every act is a physical interaction. ## API (`UF.Colonists`)
- `list()` → the colonist units (`data.kind === "colonist"`); `get(unitId)` → the unit or `null`; `isColonist(unit)`.
- `state()` → `UF.World.state.colony` (below) or `null`; `faction()` → the player's faction record; `site()` → `{ x, y, id, name, radius, area }` of the home site; `culture()` → the `catalog.cultures[species]` entry of the player's faction (`{ plan, wall, priorities, facetBias }`).
- `describe(unitOrId)` → `{ id, name, gender, mood, moodScore, job (UF.Jobs.describe text or "Idle"), jobType, needs: {hunger, thirst, sleep, social, nature} (rounded), tier, tool (item name|null), clothes (item name|null), faction, site, thought (latest text), facets, skills, plan (text) }`.
- `order(unitId, { type, target?: {x, y, area?}, params? }, onArrival?)` → the job or `null`. Cancels the colonist's current job ("ordered elsewhere") and creates the new one with `owner = unitId`; `onArrival` runs when it's done.
- `addThought(unit, text, strength)` → the thought `{ text, strength, hour, tick }`; keeps 8, moves `moodScore` (−100..100) and `mood` (Ecstatic ≥ 50, Happy ≥ 25, Content ≥ 10, Fine ≥ −10, Unhappy ≥ −25, Stressed ≥ −50, else Miserable); emits `colonists:thought`.
- `setTier(unit, tier)` → bool. Sets `data.tier`; when `data.tiers` exists, `image.characterName = tiers[min(tier, last)]` and `UF.World.refreshUnitImage`; emits `colonists:tier`. UF_Jobs' `equip` calls it for clothing.
- `planStatus()` → `[{ id, done, detail }]` evaluated from the world now (and written back to `state.colony.plan[i].done`); `planText()` → "Hearth: built · Knives: 1/4 · Shelter: 2/13 · …".
- `decide(unit)` → the job chosen now or `null` (what the loop does for an idle colonist); `tickNeeds()` (one needs tick); `setup(state)` (what `world:created` does); `setEnabled(on)` / `isEnabled()`: switches the decision loop off (needs still tick, running jobs finish) so another system's test can keep the colonists out of its arena.
- Pure helpers for tests: `nameFor(seed, unitId, gender, taken?)`, `genderFor(seed, unitId)`, `facetsFor(seed, unitId, bias)`, `skillsFor(seed, unitId)`; `stockpiles()`; `doneLog()` (this session's finished colonist jobs: `{ id, unit, type, target, physical, plan, recipe }`).

### The decision (when a colonist has no job, at most once per 60 ticks; a `thirst`/`hunger` need 25 above its threshold interrupts a non-need job, at most once per 600 ticks)
1. **Needs** (`catalog.colony.thresholds`): thirst → `drink` at the nearest water cell with a standable land neighbour (spiral to 60 cells). Hunger (or a meal hour with hunger ≥ 30 and food in the larder) → food: cooked/plant food in the pack → `eat`; raw food in the pack with a fire within 40 → `craft` the cooking recipe, else eat it raw; a brave colonist (bravery ≥ 60, or a culture with hunt priority > 1) with prey within 15 cells → `hunt`; food in the larder, then on the ground within 30 → `fetch` (raw, with a fire) or `eat` there; else `gather` the nearest object whose action yields food (60 cells); else `hunt` prey within `huntRadius` (predators only when brave). Sleep ≥ threshold, or sleeping hours (`sleepHours` shifted up to ±2 h by discipline) with sleep > 40 → `sleep` on a free `bed` object near the site, else beside the hearth, else at the site centre (`frames` = hours until the wake hour × 3600, 4–10 h). Social ≥ threshold (25 in the evening, 19–22 h) → `talk` with an idle colonist within 40. Nature ≥ threshold → `move` next to water or a tree within 20 (nature −40 on arrival).
2. **Open designations**: `UF.Jobs.take` of the best open job in the area by `priority[type] × (1 + skill/20) × (1 + job.priority) / (1 + distance/20)`.
3. **The society plan** (`state.colony.plan`, the first 3 undone steps; the culture's `priorities` of the step's next job type pick among them, ties keep the plan's order; a lazy colonist, chance `(100 − industriousness)/400` per decision, skips this and strolls):
   - `build` step: per cell relative to the site centre: a cell already holding the object is done; a cell holding another building or ruin piece, or water, is skipped; furniture (passable objects) and the site kind's own `center` object count as done when the site already holds as many as the step needs (so an established hearth and beds are done at once; walls are cell by cell). A tree or boulder on the cell is worked away first (its first action); when the `build.items` lie on the cell → `build`; else the first missing item: carried → `haul` it there; on the ground within 80 (not on another build cell) → `haul`; else the nearest object whose action yields it (60 cells; the site's own standing pieces are never quarried) → that action; else prey that yields it → `hunt`.
   - `craft` step: `each` = every colonist holds the output (equip steps: wears it, or clothes of a higher tier); `count` = the colony holds that many (packs + ground within the site). Inputs: fetch the nearest ground item, else the yielding object's action, else prey; then `craft` (recipes with `at` stand by the workplace: UF_Jobs); with `equip: true` an `equip` job follows the craft. Before a plan job the best carried tool for that job type is equipped (an `equip` job).
   - `stock` step: needs a stockpile whose `stores` include the step's first tag (the larder); carried food → cook if raw with a fire, else `haul` to the larder; food on the ground (not in the larder) → `fetch` raw to cook, else `haul`; else the nearer of the nearest food plant (`gather`) and, with `hunt: true`, the nearest prey (distance divided by the hunt priority and by 1.5 with a knife).
   - Someone else's active job on the same target (or the same recipe of the same step, the same item, the same prey) is not taken; a job that fails at creation isn't retried on that target for 900 ticks.
   - **Walled sites:** when the target is on the other side of the home site's ring, a `move` to the nearest opening in the ring comes first (`params.via`), then the same decision is taken again from there.
4. **Idle**: a seeded roll: explore (`move` to a walkable cell within 30 of the site, chance `curiosity/2`), stroll within 12 of the site (chance 0.2), or a thought.

Home: cooking happens at the colony's own hearth (the fire object at the home site), never at another faction's; a colonist farther than 40 cells from it walks home before a cooking craft, and any colonist more than 45 cells from the site (after a long chase) walks home before anything but a need (`params.home`).

Needs tick every 60 ticks by `catalog.colony.needs` (a sleeping colonist's sleep need falls instead). Skills: `jobsDone` counts per job type and per skill; one skill point per five jobs of the kind (`chop` woodcutting; `gather`/`pick` gathering; `quarry`/`mine` stonework; `build` building; `haul`/`fetch` hauling; `hunt` hunting; `craft` = the recipe's `skill`, else crafting), cap 20. Thoughts: drinking +8, eating +8, sleeping +10, talking +8, a kill +8, a building finished +10, clothes finished +6, the first woven wrap worn +15, a plan step done +12, a walk in nature +8; hunger > 75 −5, thirst > 75 −6, sleep > 85 −7, social > 80 −5, nature > 80 −3 (seeded chances per needs tick); nothing to eat −4.

## State it saves
- `UF.World.state.colony = { version, factionId, siteId, site: {x, y}, area: {x, y}, radius, plan: [{ ...step, done: bool | [cellIndex…], celebrated? }], stockpiles: [{ x, y, stores: [tags], step? }], log: [{ tick, text }], adopted }`. `plan` is a copy of `catalog.colony.plan` (or `colony.plans[culture.plan]`) with `wall_wood`/`wall_stone` replaced by the culture's `wall`. `stockpiles` gets the site's own stockpiles once the map is up (the plan's `stores` steps take the nearest ones) and every stockpile the colonists build.
- `unit.data` of a colonist: `kind: "colonist"`, `ai: "colonist"`, `faction` (the player's faction id), `species`, `sight: 8`, `gender`, `tier`, `tiers?` (humans: `catalog.start.pair[gender].tiers`), `facets {name: 0-100}`, `skills {name: 0-20}`, `needs {hunger, thirst, sleep, social, nature}`, `inventory`, `equipment {tool, clothes}`, `workRate: 1`, `thoughts`, `moodScore`, `mood`, `jobsDone`, `home`, `site`.
- Nothing else: decision timers, the avoid list and the done log are caches.

## Known limits
- 2026-09-19 addition: when `UF_FireSafety` is enabled, the scan can replace routine work with a preflighted local douse job, and idle planning clears unowned natural fuel from a hearth's four neighbors. New hearth construction waits for physical clearance; unsafe neighboring structures are not demolished. Successful dousing counts as a physical fire-state change. `Households.structures` protects annex materials and exempts annex residents from the camp leash.
- The contract's `tools_and_clothes` window is 4 real minutes; the harness watchdog is 180 s, so the suite gives it 85 s at ×8 and reports how far it got (both runs reached it in 21–60 s).
- The tier-0 sheets (`$Adam`/`$Eve`) are the unclothed pair placeholders, so an established human town starts with unclothed colonists until they weave wraps. Other species have no tier sheets and never change image.
- Colonists take open designations in their area and change the world while other suites run in the same live world: the `jobs` suite lost a hare's meat to a hungry colonist and its "far" designation to another (3 of its checks); `UF.Colonists.setEnabled(false)` is the switch for that.
- `via` moves solve the home site's ring; other enclosures rely on the 200-node pathfinder and UF_Jobs' two-stall failure.
- The `hunts` check credits the hunter's first hunt of any prey (the kit herd or a passing herd is often nearer than the test hare) and removes the hunter's carried food and nearby raw food as setup; it says so in its detail.
- UF_Test's `waitUntil` keeps a timed-out tester in its frame loop; the suite guards every wait with a liveness flag (`until`) so a stale closure can't reassign the job it watched.
- Food value isn't weighed (a hare and a deer count the same); no cooking of fish unless the catalog's `cook_fish` matches (it does, by input type).
- Skills change nothing yet except the designation score and the plan ordering weight (job speed is UF_Jobs' `workRate` × tools).

## Minimal autonomous job-taking loop (2026-09-22, DEUS-TSK-FABLE-03)

Objective 2 (2026-09-22, commit 0544ef0) wiped `decide`, `scan`, needs, moods, wandering and society-plan crafting. DEUS-TSK-FABLE-03 restored only job taking, so the open jobs `DEUS_Projects` posts get workers without console orders. Everything else Objective 2 removed stays removed.

- `scan()` runs from the `Game_Map.update` alias. Per update it costs one comparison unless a colonist's job just ended (`jobs:done` / `jobs:failed` put the worker in a pending set; it decides on the next update) or a sweep is due: every `SWEEP_EVERY` (30) ticks over the cached colonist list, at most `MAX_DECIDE_PER_SWEEP` (4) idle workers, each at most once per `DECIDE_EVERY` (60) ticks while idle. All counts are map updates (domain: action). No per-frame iteration over units, jobs or cells.
- `decide(u)`: dependants (age < 15) and busy workers decide nothing. Step 1, acute survival (`hp <= 25 %` of `maxHp`, or a need 35 above its catalog threshold and at least 90) yields to `needJob`, which returns null under Objective 2. Steps 2 to 5: `projectJob(u)` (open jobs of active `UF.Projects` projects on the worker's level, scored by the culture's priority for the type, skill, the job's priority and distance; a target or item another worker reserved, or one this worker failed on within `AVOID_TICKS`, is skipped; `UF.Jobs.take` dry-runs the plan so a job the worker can't do stays open), then `designationJob(u)` (any other open designation), then `stepOffReserved(u)` (an idle worker standing on a project's reserved square walks to the nearest free unreserved cell so a build there isn't held up). Nothing else: no strolling, no crafting, no needs.
- **Society plan deconfliction.** While `UF.Projects` is loaded and enabled (`projectsManaged()`), `societyPlan(c)` drops the plan steps whose `society` or `id` is `shelter`, `door`, `beds` or `chest`; `effectivePlan`, `planStatus`, `planText` and the build-cell index read through it. The saved `colony.plan` is untouched, and disabling `UF.Projects` brings the steps back. `DEUS_Projects` is the single source of settlement construction intent.
- Checks: `node tools/test_autonomous_project_dispatch.js` (real Objects, Items, Jobs, Projects and Colonists plugins in a vm against a World double; the driver only calls `Game_Map.update`): `plugins_load`, `founders_recognised`, `legacy_plan_bypassed`, `bypass_conditional`, `project_opened`, `claimed_autonomously` (eight founders hold eight project jobs within 120 updates), `no_double_claim` (distinct jobs and cells, every target reserved for its worker and against the other seven), `idle_scan_bounded` (at most 20 `UF.Jobs.open` calls in 120 updates of eight idle founders with nothing to do), `project_jobs_preferred` (a nearer non-project designation stays open while the eight hold project jobs), `failed_job_returns_to_pool`, `site_cleared_by_founders`, `hauls_claimed_in_walls_phase`, `no_legacy_shelter_jobs`, `no_errors`. Rule 4: `--mutant=decide_null|ignore_projects|no_bypass|per_frame` each make the run FAIL.
- Known limits: founders named `TEST_*` are never simulated (`isSettler`), as before. A worker that reaches a job's stand cell through DEUS_World's real pathing may stall; UF_Jobs' two-stall failure and the avoid list return it to the pool. ## Survival needs, SRD 5.1 model (2026-09-23, DEUS-TSK-FABLE-05; owner directive "I want it like SRD")

Objective 1 removed needs from the creature menus. The physical loop is back as the SRD writes it, not as meters. Sources: `srd:rule:adventuring-the-environment` (Food and Water, pp. 86–87), `srd:condition:exhaustion` (p. 358), `srd:rule:adventuring-resting`, `srd:rule:combat-damage-and-healing` (Dropping to 0 Hit Points), all at readiness `parsed` in `game/data/srd51/`.

- **The record** (`unit.data.needs`, replaced on sight if it still holds meters): `{ model: "srd", day, foodLb, waterGal, daysWithoutFood, exhaustion, fromNeeds, lastRestDay }`. `tickNeeds()` runs every `NEEDS_EVERY` (60) ticks over the cached colonist list and does one thing: when the calendar day (`$ufTime` year, month, day) has changed, it runs the day's reckoning for that colonist. Nothing rises per tick.
- **Intake.** A finished `eat` adds the item's nutritional contribution in pounds of the day's food (catalog `food.nutrition`, from `tools/add_srd_food_data.js`; without it the item's weight, else a fifth of a pound) and its water contribution in gallons (`food.water`); a finished `drink` adds a gallon. Weight, nutrition and water are separate numbers: one ration (2 lb) feeds the whole day, a berry feeds 0.1 lb and 0.02 gal. A colonist drinks once and eats until the day's pound is reached, then stops.
- **The day's reckoning** (`endOfDay`): a pound of food resets `daysWithoutFood`; half a pound adds half a day; less adds a day. Past 3 + Constitution modifier days (minimum 1; `data.stats.con`, default 10) the day adds one level of exhaustion. Water: a gallon a day, two while `UF.Environment` reports the colonist overheated or in heatstroke. Less than half: one level; at least half: a seeded d20 + Constitution modifier against DC 15, a level on failure; two levels either way when already exhausted. Then the day's food and water counters reset.
- **Exhaustion** is the single penalty ladder, kept in `needs.exhaustion` (0–6) with `fromNeeds` counting the levels hunger and thirst caused. `Colonists.exhaustionEffects(u)` gives other systems what to apply: level 1 disadvantage on ability checks, 2 speed halved, 3 disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws, 4 hit point maximum halved, 5 speed 0, 6 death. This plugin applies what it owns: at 5 the colonist's job is cancelled (`survival: exhaustion`) and it takes no work, only the rest; at 6 it dies (`data.dead`, hit points 0, `colonists:died`, removed from the world). Speed, attack and hit-point maximum effects are exposed, not applied, because movement, combat and hit points live in `DEUS_World`, `DEUS_Combat` and `DEUS_Dnd5e`.
- **Working unless it physically can't.** `decide(u)`: unconscious at 0 hit points (`data.hp <= 0`) takes nothing; otherwise `needJob` first, then at exhaustion 5 nothing, else project work or a designation. No fraction-of-hit-points rule remains.
- **Interruption** (`urgent(u)` in the sweep, at most once per `PREEMPT_EVERY` (600) ticks per worker, never for a need known to be unmeetable): unconscious, exhaustion 5, bedtime (sleeping hours without a long rest today), and supper (from the last catalog meal hour, 19:00, until bedtime) while the day's water or food is still short. The job is cancelled `survival: <need>`; `UF_Jobs.cancel` drops a carried stack at the worker's feet and releases its reservations.
- **`needJob(u)`** returns a real `UF_Jobs` job for what the day still needs: the long rest (bedtime, or any hour when exhausted and not yet rested today), then water (`drink` at the nearest water cell with a standable bank), then food (`foodJob`: pack, larder and containers, ground, forageable plant, prey). A need nothing can meet leaves one thought and an `avoid` entry (`need_<need>` at the hearth) for `NEED_RETRY_TICKS` (600); the colonist keeps working meanwhile.
- **The long rest** (`longRestJob`): 8 game hours (8 × 3600 ticks) in an unoccupied permitted bed, else beside the hearth, else where the colonist stands (always where it stands at speed 0). At its end (`completeLongRest`): all hit points back when it had at least one, `lastRestDay` set, and one level of exhaustion off with some food and drink that day, or, when any level came from hunger or thirst, only with the full day's pound and gallon (SRD: that exhaustion "can't be removed until the character eats and drinks the full required amount").
- Checks: `node tools/test_survival_needs_loop.js` (real Objects, Items, Jobs, Projects and Colonists plugins in a vm; the driver only calls `Game_Map.update` and moves `$ufTime`): `needs_srd_record`, `no_meter_drift`, `drinks_a_gallon_a_day`, `eats_a_pound_a_day`, `supper_preempts_labor` (a hauler carrying logs at 19:00 with no food eaten: cancelled `survival: hunger`, logs at its feet, nothing lost), `labor_resumes_after_supper`, `day_end_food_counts`, `starvation_exhaustion`, `water_rules`, `exhaustion_effects_ladder`, `level5_stops_labor`, `level6_kills`, `long_rest_rules`, `unreachable_water_no_thrash`, `no_errors`. Rule 4: `--mutant=no_preempt|free_food|no_day_end|thrash` each make the run FAIL.
- Limits: the central chest path runs through `UF.Containers` in-engine only. Catalog food has no SRD weights yet (0.2 lb each by default; SRD rations are 2 lb a day). Hot weather needs `UF.Environment`. Movement speed, attack rolls and hit-point maximum under exhaustion are for World, Combat and Dnd5e to read from `exhaustionEffects`. Newborn and immigrant records still start as meters and are migrated on their first tick.

## Dying and first aid, SRD 5.1 (2026-09-23; owner directive, task 3 of the survival order)

Source: `srd:rule:combat-damage-and-healing`, Dropping to 0 Hit Points and Stabilizing a Creature (pp. 96–99), readiness `parsed`.

- **Ownership.** `DEUS_Colonists` owns a colonist's dying state; `DEUS_Combat` (hand-off `docs/handoffs/HANDOFF_srd_dying_and_stabilization.md`) must leave a colonist at 0 hit points on the map instead of killing it, except on massive damage, and report damage at 0 through `Colonists.woundedAtZero`.
- **Unconscious.** `unconscious(u)` is `data.hp <= 0`. Every sweep starts the dying record on any unconscious colonist, idle or busy: `data.dying = { successes, failures, stable, since, nextRoundAt, wakeAt }`; its job is cancelled `survival: unconscious`; it decides nothing.
- **Death saving throws.** Every round (`ROUND_TICKS` 360, a 6 s round) an unstable dying colonist rolls a seeded d20: 10 or more a success, less a failure, a 1 two failures, a 20 one hit point and consciousness. Three successes: stable. Three failures: death (`dieOf(u, "wounds")`: colony log, `colonists:died`, then `Combat.onUnitDeath` when Combat is loaded, else removal). `Colonists.woundedAtZero(u, critical)` adds one failure, two on a critical hit, and ends stability.
- **First aid.** `Colonists.stabilize(patient, rescuer)`: d20 + Wisdom modifier (`data.stats.wis`, default 10) + 2 with `data.proficiencies` containing `"medicine"`, against DC 10; success makes the patient stable (`colonists:stabilized`), failure leaves a thought. **Stable means no longer dying, not healed**: hit points stay 0, no more saving throws, and after 1d4 hours (`wakeAt`) the colonist regains 1 hit point and consciousness (`colonists:conscious`) unless healed first.
- **The emergency aid job.** `UF_Jobs` type `stabilize` `{ unitId }` (defined in `DEUS_Jobs.js`): the rescuer reserves the patient (`ReservationManager` key `entity:<id>`, so one rescuer per patient), walks adjacent, spends one round, and calls `Colonists.stabilize`; a failed check means another round (`"continue"`). `rescueJob(u)` gives it to the nearest capable colonist for the nearest unstable patient of its faction within `RESCUE_RADIUS` (40). Priority: in `decide` it comes before the daily needs and all labour, after the rescuer's own incapacity (unconscious, exhaustion 5). In the sweep, a patient nobody is on the way to pulls the nearest idle colonist into a decision, or cuts the nearest ordinary job with `emergency: aid` (once per `PREEMPT_EVERY` per worker; need jobs and other aid are never cut).
- Checks: `node tools/test_stabilization.js`: `unconscious_starts_dying`, `rescue_job_emergency`, `medicine_check_stabilizes`, `stable_not_healed`, `stable_skips_death_saves`, `wakes_with_one_hit_point`, `death_saves_by_the_round` (the plugin's rounds match the seeded d20 sequence recomputed by the harness), `damage_at_zero_fails_saves`, `rescue_priority`, `no_errors`. Rule 4: `--mutant=no_rescue|heal_on_stable|no_death_saves|instant_death` each make the run FAIL.
- Limits: until Combat adopts the hand-off, combat damage still kills a colonist outright, so in the live game only a non-combat drop to 0 (none exists yet) enters this flow. Medicine proficiency is read from `data.proficiencies`, which nothing writes yet (Dnd5e's class skill lists include Medicine). Healing is Combat's and Dnd5e's; Colonists only notices hit points above 0.
