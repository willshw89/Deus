## What the check actually counts

`DEUS_WorldGen.js:2559–2570` calls `W.buildArea(a.x,a.y)` **three times** and counts nonzero entries of the third map's `ufObjects` via `countObjects` (`:2088–2092`). No `z` is passed, so `DEUS_World.js:1249` defaults to **z=0**. This is a map-cell count, one object maximum per cell; it is not an all-level count and it is not solely natural flora. It can include WorldGen's site pieces and guaranteed kit objects. Crucially, the check runs on the current `World.state`: `World.buildArea` applies `objectDiffs[levelKey(...)]` **after** its generators (`DEUS_World.js:1303,1335–1338`). `pristineBuild`, which swaps to empty diffs and units (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:2093–2099`), is called only **after** `objects_dense` for the subsequent kit check (`:2567–2571`). Prior triage shorthand calling the density sample “pristine” was inaccurate. The existing result does not tell how many of 1,795 are generated flora, kit, sites, or live diffs.

WorldGen registers `generate` for levels **0, 1 and 2** (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:2063–2065`). Its flora loop attaches objects to the actual `UF.Levels.surfaceElevationAt(gx,gy,seed)===z` and a floor shape (`:1590–1598`). Raised-hill flora therefore belongs on its +1/+2 map and cannot contribute to this unchanged z=0 assertion. The old baseline lane's **unapproved** `.deus_worktrees/lane-baseline/tasks/ORG-0.2/lane-baseline/PROPOSAL_test_corrections.md` A7 measured a *different seed*, 1394110930: surface elevations 17,499 at z0, 21,231 at z1, and 26,806 at z2; it proposed counting surface objects across levels. That is useful evidence of the volumetric mechanism, **not** a current-seed measurement or permission to change this test's scope. Whether 2,500 means ground-only objects or all exposed surface objects remains an Owner contract question. Keep the 2,500 threshold and the existing check intact pending that ruling.

## Exact eligibility and rejection path at 40ef

`DEUS_WorldGen.js:1427–1452` classifies 65,536 cells into biome, ground, water, peak/other flags and alignment, independently of whether a plant may stand there. The printed “biomes by cells” is this whole-map histogram (`:1582–1586,2568`), not an eligible-ground histogram. The generator then computes distance to **classified/raster** water, up to four cells (`:1526–1539`). That is not necessarily the same as painted, standable water where a raised column suppresses it.

The z0 flora loop proceeds in this order (`:1575–1612`):

1. Skip an existing object, any History site disc (radius+1), start clearing, or template-painted cell (`:1563–1573,1587`).
2. Skip `FLAG_PEAK` and any chunk for which `floraAllowed` is false (`:1588–1589`). `floraAllowed` asks `World.populationAllowed`, which requires this chunk and every in-world one of its eight neighbors to be Terrain Generated (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:1351–1359`; `DEUS_World.js:575–585`). `sealAreaTerrain` prepares the area's chunks and outside ring before the loop (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:1577`; `DEUS_World.js:605–617`); whether this gate rejects any cells on this seed is **not logged**.
3. Skip unless the physical surface elevation equals the level, skip ramps, and require `shapeCodeAt(...,z)===2` (floor) (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:1590–1598`). Raised/solid/cut columns cannot be filled on the ground map merely to raise a count.
4. Build the catalog plant table for the cell's biome and alignment; cursed/blessed alignment can alter its entries (`:292–317`). Use only its `water` or `land` list (`:1599–1600`). Catalog `biomes.*.plants` entries are per-cell chances, not guaranteed quotas (`DEUS_WorldCatalog.json:379–441`).
5. For each entry in catalog order, a dry candidate can be rejected for `waterDist<=avoidWater`. Otherwise a deterministic `unit4` roll must be below `chance * patch`; `patch` uses the object's clump/noise parameters. The first hit wins, with **no cap** (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:1601–1609`). A biome may have no positive applicable table entry, or every roll may miss. A naive sum of catalog chances over all 65,536 biome cells ignores the physical and probability gates.

Only afterward do allowed old-site pieces and the guaranteed kits add objects (`:1614–1683`). The kit has its own surface/shape/clearance rules (`:1646–1665`) and does not diagnose natural flora density. `WorldGen.stats[key]` stores placed counts by object ID, and `WorldGen.lastBuild.objects` stores a post-kit total **at the end of this generator**, before later generators and World object diffs (`:1685–1689`); existing transcripts do not print the per-biome eligibility/rejection waterfall. Grok's `worldgen.peaks_region_250` saw 39,951 peak-rock tiles/region-250 cells (`GROK_results.txt:244`), consistent with a large physical-rock share, but a tile count is **not** a measured count of the exclusive `FLAG_PEAK`, surface mismatch, or floor-shape rejection branches. It must not be subtracted from 65,536 as if it were the exact eligible area.

## What a bounded diagnostic would capture, and decision boundary

When an authorized writer next instruments this in-scope generator, the lowest-risk location is a **diagnostic-only aggregate at the existing per-cell branch points in `generate` (`DEUS_WorldGen.js:1582–1612`)**, initialized once per area/level and emitted once after the build. Keep the branch order intact and count *exclusive first rejection reasons*: prior object, site, start clearing, template, peak, chunk-not-ready, surface elevation mismatch (with S=0/1/2 histogram), ramp, nonfloor shape code, empty applicable land/water table, all entries barred by water-avoidance, and eligible-with-roll-miss versus flora placed. Break down eligible cells, effective attempts and placements by biome, alignment, water/land, level and object type. Separately record pre-diff `lastBuild.objects`, added site/kit totals and the final counted `ufObjects` so live diffs are not miscalled flora. A count of rejected **entries** must be labeled separately from rejected **cells**, since several plants are tried per cell and first success stops the list. Counters must not consume RNG, reorder list entries, alter placement, or change the assertion/threshold.

That accounting would decide among three materially different paths. If many otherwise valid z0 cells roll empty under the existing catalog rates, a narrow in-scope catalog/placement adjustment might be justified only with measured per-biome effects and the existing physical restrictions preserved. If most candidate cells have S=1/2 or nonfloor z0 shape while +1/+2 surfaces have the flora, the pre-existing A7 all-surface proposal is a **contract decision**, not an automatic test correction. If chunk gating unexpectedly removes otherwise eligible cells after `sealAreaTerrain`, a specific population-gate defect might support a narrow in-scope code repair. **Existing evidence does not distinguish these paths, and no safe production repair is already supported.** Do not fill solid rock, water, site discs or templates with arbitrary objects, multiply all plant chances blindly, lower 2,500, or report a cross-level total that has not been measured on seed 1920951434. The density item has **zero targeted repair rounds**; W16 is not used in this read-only analysis; its completed result is recorded in the morning report.
