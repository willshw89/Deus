# Segment 15: water (written 2026-09-18 by Claude Code)

The nine water kinds of `game/data/UF_WorldCatalog.json` → `water.surface`, in the catalog's order (request AR-101): fresh (A1 kind 0, tile 2048), pond (kind 1, tile 2096), marsh (kind 2, tile 2144), swamp (kind 3, tile 2192), icy (kind 4, tile 2240), brackish (kind 8, tile 2432), salt (kind 10, tile 2528), deep (kind 12, tile 2624), blighted (kind 14, tile 2720). Today all nine are drawn from the stock RPG Maker `Outside_A1` sheet (`docs/ASSET_INVENTORY.md` → Ground and water tiles); no water square was cut for `art/u7_reference_squares/`, so the reference for this segment is the atlas of decoded flat shapes named in each brief. The catalog gives a water kind no display name: the look tooltip prints its id ("water: fresh"), and the names in the headings below are descriptive only.

What the engine does with a water block (`UF_WorldGen.js`, `UF_Tiles.js`, `rmmz_core.js`, read 2026-09-18): it cuts the 96×144 block into 24×24 quarter tiles and composes the 47 autotile shapes from them, counting a neighbour as "same" when it is water of any kind, so the shore ring shows only against land and two kinds meet with no line between them; the ground under a water cell joins the ground beside it, so the water tile alone draws the shore. Every catalog water kind is forced impassable (units drink standing on the bank). For the animated kinds the engine shows the three frames in the order 0, 1, 2, 1, each for 30 engine updates (about half a second), a two-second back-and-forth. Colonists "Drink here" and "Fish here" on a water cell by right-click; nothing is built or dug on water; lily pads are a separate object drawn over some kinds. No water kind has a second state: a cell never changes kind, dries, freezes or floods.

## How to work this segment
1. Style: high-resolution micro-dithered water in the manner of the reference flat water shapes, at native resolution (1 art pixel = 1 screen pixel at zoom 1). No 16×16 upscaling, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no gradients: 1-px micro-dither is the way to shade. `docs/ART_STANDARD.md` is binding.
2. Projection: flat. Water is ground: seen from straight above, no lean, no faces, no baked shadow or lit side. Only the objects, units and items standing beside it lean up-left at 45° (`docs/GUIDE_25D.md` §1); keep the surface calm enough that a leaning tree or a person reads against it.
3. Master: one 48×48 base tile per kind that wraps on both axes (column 47 continues into column 0, row 47 into row 0), laid 2 across and 3 down to fill a 96×144 A1 block with the shore ring painted over it, and that block drawn three times side by side as animation frames 0, 1 and 2 = a 288×144 strip, `art/masters/<id>.png`. Every pixel is opaque: a water block has no magenta background and no alpha (the magenta convention is for sprites and items).
4. Block layout (RPG Maker A1, the same as A2, at 48-px tiles): the top-left tile (columns 0–47, rows 0–47) is the showcase tile with the ring all round; the top-right tile (columns 48–95, rows 0–47) is the inner-corner tile with a notch at each of its four corners; the lower 2×2 square (columns 0–95, rows 48–143) has the ring on its outer border only and nothing on its internal seams at column 48 and row 96. The engine takes the interior of the lower square for open water and its ring for shores, so the base tile must be seamless or every river shows a grid.
5. The ring is the shore, drawn on the water side only: 1 px of dark wet line on the outermost row and column, then 2 px of pale shallows. Never paint grass, sand or mud into it: the engine computes the 47 autotile shapes so the ring appears only where water meets land, any of the 22 ground kinds can lie beside it, and water of any kind joins water of any other kind with no ring at all.
6. Animation: the engine shows the frames in the order 0, 1, 2, 1, each for 30 engine updates (about half a second at 60 updates a second). Frames 1 and 2 repeat frame 0 pixel for pixel except for the small highlight shifts each brief names (glints, ripples, foam or bubbles moving 1 px, then 2 px); the ring, the notches and the field dither never change between frames, or the shore will flicker.
7. Two of the nine kinds (marsh, swamp) sit in the A1 slots the engine draws from one still block and does not count as water in its cell tests (each brief says so). Deliver their three frames all the same as the others; the engine shows frame 0 until Claude Code moves them.
8. Palette: `art/palette/uf.hex` only (every index below was checked against the file on 2026-09-18); aim for ≤ 32 colours per kind, the briefs use 8–10; a micro-dither of two neighbouring indices is how a hue between them is made; no baked shadows.
9. Open the references first: `reference/u7_shapes_0_31.png` (the atlas of the decoded flat shapes 0–31, opened 2026-09-18: shape 2, row 0 third cell, is the plain open water; 19, 20 and 26 are its darker variants) and the composite `art/review/u7_square_composite.png` (the objects and people that will stand beside the water; it must sit under them, not fight them). They are references and development stand-ins: nothing copied from them ships.
10. Sidecar: `art/masters/<id>.json` with the AR-600 fields (frameWidth 96, frameHeight 144, anchor null because a tile has no anchor, facings [], animations { "surface": [0, 1, 2, 1] }) plus layer "water", the A1 kind, the tile id and the slot [x, y] given in each Deliver line.
11. Check: `"C:\Program Files\nodejs\node.exe" tools/art_check.js --type tileset art/masters/<id>.png` (for tilesets the tool skips the sidecar and its 3×-grid check does not apply to 48-native masters until it is updated; the palette check and the alpha check, every pixel 255, do apply). Export (ART_STANDARD §5 step 8, no scaling): Claude Code pastes each approved strip into one 768×576 A1 sheet at its slot (kind 0 at x 0, y 0; kind 1 at x 0, y 144; kind 2 at x 288, y 0; kind 3 at x 288, y 144; kind 4 at x 384, y 0; kind 8 at x 0, y 288; kind 10 at x 0, y 432; kind 12 at x 384, y 288; kind 14 at x 384, y 432; the two still kinds take only the first 96 px of their strip), copies it to `game/img/tilesets/` and switches `tilesets.surface.A1` in the catalog from the stock sheet to it. Gemini does not edit `game/data/`.
12. Mark the AR-101 row in `docs/ASSET_REQUESTS.md` DELIVERED with the backticked file names as strips arrive; the user approves each kind before it ships (`art/APPROVALS.md`). Order: fresh first (rivers and the start pond: the water most seen), then this file top to bottom. Verify a brief you edit with `"C:\Program Files\nodejs\node.exe" tools/check_briefs.js docs/asset_briefs/SEG-15_water.md` and fix every FAIL before reporting.
