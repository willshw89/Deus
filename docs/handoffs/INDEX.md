# Handoffs: index (written 2026-09-29 by the PM under DEC-042)

> **SUPERSEDED FOR GENERATION (2026-09-29; DEC-007 amendment).** Every art handoff in this folder was written for Google Nano Banana Pro, the 2.5D lean or the Gemini delivery procedure. None of them is a generation instruction any more. The procedure is `docs/art/DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md` §3: catalogue record, `art/prompts/<id>.json`, PixelLab OBJECTS and MAPS only, QA, review board, the Owner's SHA-256 ledger, induction, in-game check.
> The engine and SRD handoffs are unaffected by the art rules; they are task records for the systems they name and stay as written.
> Where a handoff names a generator, a camera, a palette, a sheet layout or a status word, `docs/art/DEUS_ASSET_STANDARD.md` wins (AS-GEN-004, AS-VIEW-002, AS-GLOBAL-004, AS-GLOBAL-012, §3.6).
> Do not update the handoffs. A new asset need becomes a catalogue record, not a new `HANDOFF_<feature>.md`.
